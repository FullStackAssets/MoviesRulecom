On November 20, Hulu released the first trailer for Only Murders in the Building Season 6, showing the beloved trio—Charles, Oliver, and Mabel—traveling to London to investigate the murder of podcaster Cinda Canning. The teaser highlights a new setting and a host of high‑profile guest stars, including Tina Fey, Meryl Streep, and David Tennant. Season 6 is slated to premiere on December 8, promising fresh twists for fans of the comedy‑crime hybrid. The trailer’s star‑studded lineup signals a broader appeal beyond the show’s core audience. It also hints at a deeper narrative that could reshape the series’ direction.

## What happened
The Hulu trailer shows Charles, Oliver, and Mabel arriving in London to investigate the murder of fellow podcaster Cinda Canning. They expect a different world, but the city’s eccentricities mirror their New York life more than anticipated. The teaser also showcases a lineup of guest stars such as Tina Fey, Olivia Colman, Meryl Streep, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan, and Martin Freeman.

The season’s executive producers include the original creators Martin and John Hoffman, along with Short, Gomez, Dan Fogelman, Jess Rosenthal, Ben Smith, Jennifer Crittenden, and Gabrielle Allan. The official synopsis describes the trio navigating a new set of suspicious, English‑eccentric characters while trying to prevent their own story from ending like Cinda’s.

## Why it matters

NBC News Now announced the abrupt cancellation of its primetime streaming series Stay Tuned, the network’s only original show produced out of Los Angeles. The decision, confirmed by a source close to the network, will see the final episode air on Wednesday and the timeslot filled with reruns. Around a dozen staff members are losing their positions, though they may apply for roughly 50 openings elsewhere in NBC News. The move reflects a broader reassessment of resources as the platform chases growth after a strong election‑night viewership surge.

## What happened
NBC News Now has dropped Stay Tuned, the only primetime series originating from Los Angeles, with the final episode airing Wednesday. Anchor Gadi Schwartz will stay with the network, while about a dozen staffers face job changes but can apply for roughly 50 internal openings, including roles on Dateline and news specials. The network cited a resource and programming review despite recent viewer growth, and Variety was the first to report the move.

The cancellation ends a show launched in 2023 that expanded from a Snapchat series co‑hosted by Schwartz and Savannah Sellers. NBC News Now will fill the vacant slot with reruns of existing programs while prioritizing its broader streaming strategy.

## Why it matters
The loss of a West Coast‑based original series reduces regional production diversity and signals that NBC News Now is tightening its content slate to focus on higher‑performing assets. Staff displacement highlights the human cost of rapid strategic pivots in digital news. At the same time, the network’s emphasis on reallocating resources may accelerate growth in other digital news offerings, reshaping the streaming news landscape.

The Final Problem dropped on Netflix on September 25, 2026, a Spanish period mystery that feels like Knives Out meets Only Murders in the Building. It follows semi‑retired actor Benjamin Basil, once famed for playing Sherlock Holmes, who travels to a remote Mallorca island and gets pulled into a murder investigation when guest Elisa Mander is found dead. The series blends a 1959 setting, locked‑room tension, and a witty ensemble, positioning it as a fresh entry in the streaming mystery boom and a potential new go‑to for fans of clever whodunits.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Final Problem proves that a well‑crafted, character‑driven whodunit can thrive on streaming platforms.</Callout>

## What happened
Netflix released The Final Problem, a Spanish series adapted from Arturo Pérez‑Reverte’s novel, on September 25, 2026. The story centers on Benjamin Basil (José Coronado), a former star of Sherlock Holmes films who hopes to revive his career by visiting a secluded hotel on an island near Mallorca.

When quiet English tourist Elisa Mander is discovered dead, what appears to be a suicide quickly turns into a murder. A storm forces the hotel into lockdown, isolating the guests and staff while Basil steps into his detective role, interrogating each suspect and piecing together clues hidden in the 1959 setting.

The series has generated buzz for its stylish period design, tight plotting, and the way it balances homage to classic whodunits with fresh, character‑driven humor.

## Why it matters
The Final Problem signals Netflix’s commitment to high‑quality international productions that can compete with Hollywood‑style mysteries. By marrying a nostalgic 1950s backdrop with modern storytelling, the series expands the streaming catalog’s genre diversity and offers English‑speaking audiences a compelling non‑English option. Its success could encourage more adaptations of literary thrillers from Spain and other markets, broadening representation on a global platform.

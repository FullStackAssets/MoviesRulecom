Anne Hathaway’s name was added to the Santa Barbara Film Festival’s prestigious roster of honorees on November 12, 2026. The festival announced she will receive the 2027 American Riviera Award, a tribute that celebrates actors who have shaped American cinema. Her in‑person conversation is slated for February 5, where she’ll discuss her career highlights, including her role as Penelope in Christopher Nolan’s billion‑dollar‑grossing epic *The Odyssey*. The award places her among a lineage of celebrated talent such as Ethan Hawke, Zoe Saldaña, and Robert Redford. This recognition arrives as the festival also unveiled its Virtuoso Awards, underscoring a broader celebration of contemporary film artistry.

## What happened
The Santa Barbara Film Festival confirmed that Hathaway will be honored with the American Riviera Award during a live tribute on February 5. The ceremony will feature a conversation that spotlights her performance in *The Odyssey*, a film that has already surpassed the billion‑dollar mark worldwide. In addition to Nolan’s epic, the announcement highlighted her recent work in *The Devil Wears Prada 2* alongside Meryl Streep and the sci‑fi survival picture *The End of Oak Street*.

Festival Executive Director Roger Durling praised Hathaway, saying she “delivers a breathtaking performance… that stands among the finest of her career.” The award joins a distinguished list that includes Ethan Hawke, Zoe Saldaña, Mark Ruffalo, and many other luminaries who have previously been recognized for their contributions to American cinema.

## Why it matters

The festival’s spotlight can boost viewership for her recent films, especially *The Odyssey*, which benefits from renewed buzz ahead of its awards season run. Moreover, the accolade enhances the festival’s reputation as a platform that celebrates both established and emerging talent.

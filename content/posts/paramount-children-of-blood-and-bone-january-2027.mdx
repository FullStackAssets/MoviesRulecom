Paramount has finally set a release date for its upcoming fantasy adaptation Children of Blood and Bone, slated for January 2027. The film brings Tomi Adeyemi’s bestselling novel to the big screen, starring Thuso Mbedu as the magic‑wielding heroine Zélie. With the announcement arriving just ten weeks before the official trailer drop, the studio is signaling a major push into original fantasy franchises. This move positions Paramount alongside Netflix and Sony, which have recently unveiled their own high‑profile fantasy projects. Fans of YA adaptations and new cinematic universes are watching closely.

<Callout type="takeaway">Paramount’s Children of Blood and Bone will be the studio’s flagship fantasy launch in early 2027.</Callout>

## What happened
Paramount confirmed that Children of Blood and Bone will open in theaters in January 2027, making it the first major fantasy property the studio has green‑lit since its 2024 slate. The announcement came alongside a teaser poster featuring Thuso Mbedu as Zélie Adebola, with production reportedly underway in South Africa and the United Kingdom.

The adaptation follows a seven‑year journey from Fox 2000’s original option to Paramount’s acquisition, and it will be directed by a yet‑to‑be‑named filmmaker with a background in genre storytelling. Alongside Mbedu, the cast includes rising talent and veteran actors who will portray the royal siblings that ally with Zélie, underscoring the studio’s commitment to a diverse ensemble.

## Why it matters
Paramount’s move signals a strategic shift toward original, high‑concept fantasy that can compete with Netflix’s rapid rollout of YA adaptations and Sony’s blockbuster game‑based releases. By anchoring its 2027 lineup with a culturally resonant story from a Black author, the studio aims to capture both domestic and international audiences hungry for fresh mythologies. The timing also aligns with the broader industry trend of building franchise pipelines ahead of the next wave of streaming‑theater hybrid windows.

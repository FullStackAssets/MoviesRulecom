Magnolia Pictures just secured U.S. distribution for the Cannes Midnight animated comedy 'Jim Queen', a bold LGBTQ satire fronted by Jordan Firstman. The film follows gay influencer Jim as a mysterious virus threatens to turn gay men straight, turning his Pride‑royal status into social exile. With a star‑studded voice cast that includes Matteo Lane, Nicole Byer, John Cameron Mitchell, Bob the Drag Queen and Wendie Malick, the movie promises a mix of humor and social commentary. Its summer theatrical rollout marks a rare mainstream push for adult‑oriented queer animation.

## What happened
Magnolia Pictures announced it has acquired U.S. rights to 'Jim Queen', the Cannes Midnight animated comedy created by Marco Nguyen and Nicolas Athané. The English‑language version, co‑directed by Wolfie Trausch, will hit theaters this summer, according to the studio’s co‑CEOs.

The film stars Jordan Firstman as the titular gay influencer Jim, alongside Matteo Lane, Nicole Byer, John Cameron Mitchell, Bob the Drag Queen and Wendie Malick. Executive producers include musician Moby and actress Lindsay Hicks, while French studio Bobbypills handles animation.

The plot centers on a virus called Heterosis that begins turning gay men straight, forcing Jim to rally his remaining allies and search for a cure before the community is erased.

## Why it matters

Magnolia’s theatrical push also tests the market viability of adult‑oriented queer animation against traditional summer blockbusters, potentially shaping future acquisition strategies.

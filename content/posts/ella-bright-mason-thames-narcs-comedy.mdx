Ella Bright is set to share the screen with Mason Thames in Sony’s upcoming coming‑of‑age comedy ‘Narcs’. The duo was announced shortly after Bright wrapped season 2 of the Netflix series Off‑Campus. ‘Narcs’ follows two high‑school hall monitors who enlist an ex‑popular girl to crash a massive party, a premise that echoes hits like Booksmart. The film is slated for a theatrical debut on August 20, 2027. Anticipation is high as both stars bring recent box‑office momentum to the project.

## What happened
Sony announced that Ella Bright and Mason Thames will lead the new comedy ‘Narcs’, directed by John Phillips, the writer behind No Hard Feelings. The script was developed by Jordan & Justin Shipley, John Phillips, and Gene Stupnitsky, promising a blend of sharp humor and teenage authenticity. The movie is scheduled to open in theaters on August 20, 2027.

Bright recently finished season 2 of the Netflix series Off‑Campus, where she plays a college freshman navigating campus life. Thames has built a strong résumé with roles in How to Train Your Dragon, Black Phone 2, and the hit series Regretting You, becoming the first actor since Jim Carrey to top the box office with three films in a single year. Their combined recent success adds significant buzz to the project.

The supporting cast includes Callan Farris, Lexi Minetree, and Josh Macqueen, all familiar faces from Prime Video originals. With Sony backing and a summer 2027 release window, the studio aims to position ‘Narcs’ as a flagship teen comedy for the next generation.

## Why it matters
‘Narcs’ arrives at a moment when teen comedies are enjoying a resurgence, and its premise taps into the nostalgic yet fresh vibe that resonated with audiences in Booksmart and Lady Bird. For Bright, the film marks a transition from streaming series to a major theatrical lead, potentially expanding her fan base beyond Netflix. Thames’ involvement reinforces his momentum as a box‑office draw, signaling Sony’s confidence in pairing rising talent with proven commercial appeal. The movie also highlights Sony’s strategy to diversify its slate with youth‑focused content ahead of the 2027 summer season.

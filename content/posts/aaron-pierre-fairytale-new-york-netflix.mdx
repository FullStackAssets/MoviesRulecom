Netflix has secured the Aaron Pierre action‑thriller 'Fairytale in New York' after a competitive bid with Lionsgate. The film, directed by Jalmari Helander, follows a cab driver on a vengeful night in Christmas‑Eve Manhattan. Pierre, known for his turn in HBO's 'Lanterns', leads the cast alongside a script by Rory Haines and Sohrab Noshirvani. The deal signals Netflix’s push for fresh, character‑driven action content.
<Callout type="takeaway">Netflix’s acquisition adds a high‑concept action thriller to its holiday lineup.</Callout>
## What happened
Netflix outbid other bidders to acquire the feature from Lionsgate, securing worldwide streaming rights. Helander, the director of 'Sisu', will helm the project, which was written as an original spec script by Haines and Noshirvani. Production will be overseen by Beau Flynn and Kevin King Templeton, with executive producers including Scott Sheldon.
The film stars Aaron Pierre as John Stewart, a cab driver whose estranged son’s priceless gift is stolen on Christmas Eve, prompting a relentless quest for vengeance. Pierre’s recent work includes the Netflix film 'Rebel Ridge' and the HBO series 'Lanterns', positioning him for a breakout leading role.
## Why it matters
For Netflix, the acquisition expands its slate of original action‑thrillers that can attract both genre fans and broader audiences during the holiday season. Pierre’s rising profile gives the platform a fresh star with proven appeal, while Lionsgate continues to strengthen its partnership with the streaming giant after successes like 'War Machine'. The film’s gritty, character‑focused style aligns with Netflix’s strategy to blend high‑octane set‑pieces with emotional depth.

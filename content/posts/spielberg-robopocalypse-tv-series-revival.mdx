## What happened
Collider reported that the long‑abandoned film adaptation of Daniel H. Wilson’s novel *Robopocalypse* is officially moving forward as a television series. Ron Howard is attached to direct the pilot episode, and Akiva Goldsman will serve as writer and showrunner. The project is being developed by Howard’s Imagine Entertainment and Goldsman’s Weed Road, with Spielberg’s Amblin Entertainment listed as an executive producer.

The novel imagines a world where a childlike artificial intelligence called Archos comes online, gains massive power, and turns against its creators, sparking a global conflict. Translating the premise to a serialized format gives creators room to explore the societal collapse, resistance movements, and moral dilemmas that a two‑hour film could only hint at.

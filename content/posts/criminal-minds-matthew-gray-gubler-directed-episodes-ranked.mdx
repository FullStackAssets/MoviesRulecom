## What happened
Matthew Gubler stepped behind the camera for twelve Criminal Minds installments, beginning with the Season 9 episode “The Silencer” and concluding with “The Capilanos” in Season 13. His episodes often explore darker psychological themes, such as the clown‑themed killings in “The Capilanos” or the family‑trauma‑driven crimes in “The Capilanos” sequel. While he continued acting as Reid, Gubler used his director’s chair to experiment with visual symbolism, lingering shots, and heightened tension.

Beyond the BAU, Gubler’s directing résumé includes a handful of indie projects, but his television work remains the most visible. After Criminal Minds ended its original run in 2020, he appeared in the film Horse Girl (2020) and the series Dollface (2021), yet his directorial output has slowed, making this collection of twelve episodes a concise showcase of his style.

Critics note that episodes like “The Capilanos” and “The Night Stalker” stand out for their atmospheric dread, while others such as “The Unsub” receive mixed feedback for pacing. The ranking considers narrative cohesion, visual flair, and how each episode contributes to the series’ larger mythos.

Netflix unveiled its latest limited series, The Altruists, on Thursday, November 19. The eight‑episode drama follows the rise and fall of the FTX cryptocurrency empire, centering on CEO Caroline Ellison and founder Sam Bankman‑Fried. Julia Garner headlines the cast, marking her first Netflix project since Inventing Anna. The show blends a true‑crime narrative with a romance between the two central figures. Its release taps into ongoing public interest in crypto fraud and the moral questions of effective altruism.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Altruists dramatizes an $8 B crypto fraud through the intertwined lives of its founders, offering a fresh lens on the FTX collapse.</Callout>

## What happened

The series premiered on Netflix on November 19, 2026, and consists of eight episodes. It was co‑created by Oscar‑winning screenwriter Graham Moore and executive‑produced by Barack and Michelle Obama, adding to their streaming slate. Julia Garner portrays Caroline Ellison, the former CEO of Alameda Research, while Anthony Boyle plays Sam Bankman‑Fried, the co‑founder of FTX.

The narrative follows the rapid ascent of FTX and Alameda, the intertwined romance between Ellison and Bankman‑Fried, and the eventual collapse that saw more than $8 B of customer funds disappear. The show is based on real‑life events surrounding the 2022 FTX bankruptcy and the subsequent investigations. It also explores the concept of effective altruism that motivated Bankman‑Fried’s philanthropic ambitions.

Supporting roles include Hudson Williams as a regulatory investigator, Matt Rife as a hedge‑fund manager, and Alex Lawther as a venture capitalist, all contributing to the series’ depiction of the crypto ecosystem.

## Why it matters

The series brings a high‑profile dramatization to a complex financial scandal that shook the global crypto market. It highlights how unchecked ambition and opaque governance can erode public trust. Viewers gain insight into the human side of a crisis that affected thousands of investors.

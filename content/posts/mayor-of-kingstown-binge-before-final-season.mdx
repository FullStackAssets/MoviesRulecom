The final season of Mayor of Kingstown is on the horizon, and Netflix has just added the entire 40‑episode catalog to its library. The Paramount+ crime thriller, created by Taylor Sheridan, arrived on Netflix in the United States on October 5, giving new and returning viewers instant access. With the series’ explosive conclusion expected soon, the timing creates a perfect opportunity for a marathon. Binge‑watching now lets fans refresh storylines and character arcs before the climax unfolds.

## What happened
Taylor Sheridan’s crime drama Mayor of Kingstown secured a licensing deal earlier this year, moving the four‑season, 40‑episode run from Paramount+ to Netflix for U.S. audiences. The shift placed the series alongside other Sheridan creations that have found new life on the streaming platform, expanding its reach to Netflix’s massive subscriber base.

The show follows Jeremy Renner as Mike McLusky, a fixer navigating a town built on incarceration, gang violence, and political corruption. Over four seasons, the narrative deepens the town’s moral complexities, setting the stage for an anticipated final season that promises heightened stakes.

## Why it matters
Having the full back‑catalog on a widely used platform lowers the barrier for casual viewers to catch up, potentially boosting viewership ahead of the finale. The bingeability also fuels online discussion, increasing hype and keeping the series relevant in a crowded streaming market. For Netflix, the addition strengthens its crime‑thriller lineup, while fans gain a cohesive story experience before the concluding arc.

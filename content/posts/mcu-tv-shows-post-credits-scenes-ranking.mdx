The ranking reflects how each scene either reinforces character arcs, hints at upcoming storylines, or simply provides a comedic moment.

Conversely, scenes that feel like afterthoughts or lack clear consequences are pushed toward the bottom.
## Why it matters
Post‑credits scenes shape fan expectations and can influence how audiences perceive the continuity of the MCU.
A well‑executed scene rewards viewers and builds momentum for the next installment, while a weak one can leave fans feeling disconnected.

## How to think about it
When evaluating a post‑credits scene, consider its narrative payoff, its connection to the larger MCU, the humor it offers, and how it impacts character development.
A scene that clearly advances a plot or deepens a character is more valuable than one that merely provides a joke.
Use this framework to decide whether a scene feels earned or just a quick teaser.

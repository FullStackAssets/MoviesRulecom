The long-awaited return of the Friday the 13th franchise has finally taken shape with the announcement of its title, full cast, and release date. The 13th installment, simply titled Jason, is set to hit theaters worldwide on August 13, 2027. Directed by Steve Miner, the film marks the first new entry in the series in nearly two decades, rekindling anticipation among horror fans who have waited years for a theatrical follow-up.

<Callout type="takeaway">The franchise’s 13th film, titled Jason, premieres August 13, 2027, starring Schuyler White as the iconic masked killer in a return to the big screen after a nearly 20-year hiatus.</Callout>

## What happened

Steve Miner has been confirmed to direct the new entry, which wraps production as of November 18, 2026. The film is titled Jason and is scheduled for a worldwide theatrical release on Friday, August 13, 2027. Cineverse will handle North American distribution for the project.

The cast is led by Schuyler White, a stunt coordinator and special effects artist, in the role of Jason Voorhees. White previously portrayed the character in the short film Sweet Revenge, created to mark the 45th anniversary of the franchise. Natalie Alyn Lind, known for her role in Dutton Ranch, plays the final girl, Lauren. The ensemble also includes Matt Cornett, Lilia Buckingham, John Owen Lowe, Summer Madison, Selena Turner, Matthew Keoni Sato, Brook Sill, Guillermo Diaz, Johnell Young, Sencere M. Brown, and Bryan Gael Guzman.

## Why it matters

This release is significant because it breaks a nearly two-decade gap in the Friday the 13th theatrical lineup, signaling a renewed commitment to the franchise from its stewards. The choice to cast a stunt professional as Jason reflects a shift toward emphasizing physicality and intimidation over traditional acting, aiming to present the character as an "unstoppable force of nature." For fans, the specific release date of August 13, 2027, aligns with the franchise's legacy, while the involvement of a new generation of actors suggests an attempt to broaden the audience while honoring the original material.

## How to think about it

Approach this announcement as a reset for the franchise rather than a simple sequel. The decision to title the film Jason and cast a stuntman in the lead role indicates a focus on the character’s physical presence and horror elements over narrative complexity. Fans should watch for how the film balances the iconic imagery of the mask with the new cast’s dynamics, particularly the relationship between Jason and the final girl, Lauren. The release date in August 2027 positions the film for a strong summer horror slot, competing with other major releases but leveraging the franchise’s built-in audience.

Marvel’s biggest blockbuster gets a fresh coat of paint. On November 13, 2026 Disney+ launched Avengers: Endgame – Encore, an updated cut of the 2019 climax that adds roughly four minutes of footage and a new color‑grading palette. The limited theatrical run in September introduced the same changes to cinemas, and the streaming version now brings those extras directly to fans’ homes. This release not only revives the epic showdown with Thanos but also teases the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday storyline.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Encore cut offers a slightly longer, visually refreshed experience that expands the story while staying true to the original film.</Callout>

## What happened
The Encore version debuted in a limited theatrical engagement on September 25, 2026, opening at number one worldwide and pulling an estimated $86 million in its opening weekend.

Running about four minutes longer than the original, the cut features new color grading and additional scenes that bridge to the forthcoming Avengers: Doomsday, giving viewers fresh narrative beats.

Disney+ made the edition exclusive to its platform on November 13, 2026, positioning it as a flagship event for the service’s 2026 slate.

## Why it matters
By extending the runtime and updating the visual tone, Marvel gives longtime fans a reason to revisit a familiar story while subtly setting up plot threads for future installments. The streaming‑first strategy also reinforces Disney+ as the primary home for premium Marvel content, driving subscriber engagement ahead of the next phase of the MCU.

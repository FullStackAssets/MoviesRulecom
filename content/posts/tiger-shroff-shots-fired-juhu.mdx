Late Tuesday evening, unidentified gunmen opened fire on Bollywood star Tiger Shroff as he walked through Juhu, an upscale suburb of Mumbai. Witnesses reported a flurry of shots, but the actor escaped without visible injuries. Police arrived within minutes, cordoned off the area and launched an investigation. Shortly after, members of the Lawrence Bishnoi gang posted online claiming responsibility for the attack. The incident has sent shockwaves through the film community and raised fresh concerns about celebrity security.

<Callout type="takeaway">The shooting underscores growing threats to Bollywood personalities and the need for heightened security.</Callout>

## What happened
According to the Times of India report, gunfire was heard near Shroff’s vehicle in Juhu, prompting nearby residents to call emergency services. Mumbai police confirmed that the shots were fired from an unidentified vehicle and that no one was injured, though the actor’s entourage was shaken. Within hours, a social media post attributed the act to the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, a criminal outfit previously linked to extortion and violent retaliation in the city.

Dodongo Cavern opens with a cracked wall and a Bomb Flower just to the left, inviting players to blast the entrance and descend into the fiery lair. The dungeon quickly becomes a test of timing, as shallow lava pools and rotating Beamos lasers force careful movement. Along the way you’ll uncover the dungeon map, compass, and a Bomb Bag, each essential for progressing toward the boss.

The central chamber houses a giant skull that guards the path to the boss, but it can be accessed by bombing hidden alcoves and navigating rising platforms. After securing the key items, the final encounter with the Dodongo boss requires strategic bomb placement and quick reflexes to avoid its crushing attacks.

<Callout type="takeaway">Master the Bomb Flower and platform routes to breeze through Dodongo Cavern and claim the Spiritual Stone of Fire.</Callout>

## What happened
The walkthrough begins by tossing the Bomb Flower at the cracked wall, revealing the true entrance. Inside, players encounter two Beamos guardians; using another Bomb Flower to destroy the first Beamos clears a path to a treasure chest containing a Blue Rupee. Further exploration uncovers the dungeon map and compass hidden behind bombable rock, while a second Bomb Flower opens a southern alcove with additional loot.

Progress continues by climbing a series of lava‑surrounded platforms, timing jumps to avoid the rotating laser of the second Beamos. After collecting the Bomb Bag, you can safely navigate the central skull area, bomb the secret path, and enter the boss arena where the Dodongo awaits.

## Why it matters
Dodongo Cavern serves as the first major dungeon in the remake, teaching players core mechanics—bomb usage, platform navigation, and enemy pattern reading—that recur throughout the game. Successfully completing it not only grants the Spiritual Stone of Fire but also reinforces confidence in handling more complex challenges later.

## How to think about it
Treat the dungeon as a series of micro‑puzzles: first, secure the entry with a bomb, then map out safe platform routes before engaging enemies. Prioritize obtaining the Bomb Bag early; it reduces risk when handling the Beamos and the final boss. When you reach the skull area, pause to scan for hidden alcoves—bombing them often yields the map, compass, or extra rupees that make the boss fight smoother.

<Callout type="warning">Do not linger near the lava pools or look directly at a Beamos eye; a single misstep can send you back to the start.</Callout>

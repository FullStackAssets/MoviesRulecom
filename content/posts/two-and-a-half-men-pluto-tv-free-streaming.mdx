Pluto TV announced that the full run of the hit sitcom ‘Two and a Half Men’ will become available on its free, ad‑supported platform on November 20. The move marks the first time the 12‑season series is offered without a subscription fee, joining five other Warner Bros. TV titles in a new output deal. The addition expands Pluto TV’s FAST (Free Ad‑Supported Streaming TV) library and comes as the service reports a 70 % rise in viewership this year. For fans of classic sitcoms, the launch provides a rare chance to binge the entire 262‑episode catalog without a paywall. Industry observers see the deal as a signal that legacy TV libraries are increasingly finding homes on free streaming services.

<Callout type="takeaway">Pluto TV’s free launch of ‘Two and a Half Men’ underscores the growing value of legacy sitcoms on ad‑supported platforms.</Callout>

## What happened
Pluto TV added the sitcom as part of a broader Warner Bros. TV output pact that also brings “Veronica Mars,” “The Jamie Foxx Show,” “Martin,” and “The Wayans Bros.” All 12 seasons (262 episodes) of ‘Two and a Half Men’ will begin streaming on November 20, with the other series rolling out between November 15 and 20.

The platform has added more than 25 library titles this year, and its AVOD viewership is up 70 % year‑to‑date. In addition, Pluto TV introduced 14 FAST channels sourced from the Warner Bros. library, ranging from “Crime Scenes” to “Whose Line Is It Anyway.”

Will Gurman, Pluto TV’s senior VP of content partners and programming, said the sitcom “anchors a slate of proven hits our viewers already know and rewatch,” highlighting the strategic push toward free streaming as the fastest‑growing segment of the market.

## Why it matters
The deal gives Pluto TV a marquee anchor that can attract both nostalgic viewers and new audiences, boosting ad inventory and reinforcing the platform’s position against subscription‑heavy competitors. For Warner Bros. TV, the arrangement unlocks revenue from a catalog that has largely been tied up in legacy pay‑TV windows, while preserving the option to license the series elsewhere in the future. Viewers benefit from immediate, cost‑free access, but the ad‑supported model may affect binge‑watch satisfaction.

## How to think about it
If you’re a fan of the series, add Pluto TV’s “Two and a Half Men” channel to your lineup and set a reminder for the November 20 launch. Consider using a browser extension or TV guide to skip ads if they become intrusive, and compare the free experience with subscription options that may offer ad‑free viewing. For advertisers, the rollout presents a fresh inventory of engaged, nostalgia‑driven audiences.

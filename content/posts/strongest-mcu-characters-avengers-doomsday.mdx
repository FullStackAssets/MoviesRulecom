## What happened
The MCU has long grappled with a "power problem" as sequels demand ever‑greater stakes. Screen Rant identified ten confirmed characters for Avengers: Doomsday, ranking them by raw power. At the summit sits Sue Storm, the Invisible Woman, whose upgraded force‑field ability can briefly contain Galactus, eclipsing her Fantastic Four teammates. Magneto follows, bringing Omega‑level mutant mastery, while other heavyweights like the Young Avengers, Hawkeye, and the missing original X‑Men round out the list.

Notably, several marquee names are absent. Doctor Strange, Captain Marvel, and Spider‑Man are officially sitting Doomsday out, though rumors suggest Strange may appear as one of Doctor Doom’s Latverian witches. Their exclusion reshapes the power dynamics, forcing the narrative to lean on less‑expected heavy hitters and expanding the MCU’s roster beyond the usual suspects.

## Why it matters
The ranking signals a shift in storytelling focus: visual spectacle will hinge on characters whose abilities can scale to cosmic threats, such as force‑fields that trap Galactus. Audiences can anticipate battles that prioritize raw power over familiar hero arcs, potentially attracting viewers eager for novel super‑powered showdowns. However, sidelining beloved protagonists risks alienating fans who expect their favorite icons to drive the climax.

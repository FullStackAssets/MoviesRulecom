Netflix’s newest breakout, Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, vaulted into the platform’s Global Top 10 Non‑English Shows this week. The ten‑episode anime sequel dropped on October 20, 2026 and logged 4.6 million views in its first tracking week (Oct 19‑25). Its strong debut propelled the joint CD Projekt Red‑Studio Trigger title to briefly sit at number 2 among all Netflix series worldwide. The surge revives a franchise that fans have been waiting four years to see return, and it reshapes the streaming landscape for non‑English sci‑fi content.
<Callout type="takeaway">Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 proves that a well‑timed sequel can dominate Netflix’s global charts even in a crowded sci‑fi slate.</Callout>
## What happened
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 entered Netflix’s Global Top 10 Non‑English Shows chart at #3 immediately after its worldwide release on October 20, 2026. In its debut tracking week (Oct 19‑25) the series amassed 4.6 million views, briefly peaking as the platform’s #2 most‑watched series across all languages.
The sequel, a collaboration between CD Projekt Red and Studio Trigger, was directed by Kai Ikarashi and overseen by showrunner Bartosz Sztybor. Critics note that the creators intentionally subverted expectations, resulting in a more divided response than the universally praised 2022 original.
The announcement arrives amid a strong year for sci‑fi streaming, with titles like HBO’s Lanterns, Apple TV+’s Star City, and Disney+’s Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord also landing in top‑tier positions.
## Why it matters
The rapid climb demonstrates Netflix’s ability to leverage legacy IP and non‑English content to capture global audiences, reinforcing the platform’s strategy to diversify beyond English‑language originals. For the anime community, the success signals growing appetite for high‑budget, story‑driven sequels on streaming services. It also puts pressure on competitors to invest in comparable sci‑fi franchises, potentially reshaping licensing and production budgets for the genre.

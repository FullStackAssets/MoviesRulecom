Pocket.watch, the creator‑focused studio known for short‑form series, announced its first foray into feature‑length storytelling. The company has signed a distribution deal with wildlife filmmaker Mark Vins to bring his debut film ‘Reef To Ridge’ to YouTube and other platforms. The film, which debuted at the Toronto International Film Festival’s Creator Festival, follows an expedition across the Galápagos from sea to volcanic summit. This move signals a shift in how digital‑native studios are leveraging built‑in audiences to launch cinematic projects.

<Callout type="takeaway">Pocket.watch is using creator fanbases to launch feature films, starting with ‘Reef To Ridge’.</Callout>

## What happened
Pocket.watch teamed with Vins’ label Brave Wilderness to handle distribution for ‘Reef To Ridge’. Brave Wilderness, Vins’ YouTube channel, boasts over 21.6 million subscribers and more than 6 billion views, giving the film a ready‑made audience across digital platforms. The partnership will see the film released on YouTube while Pocket.watch pursues additional SVOD homes.

The documentary premiered at TIFF as part of the inaugural Creator Festival, offering viewers an immersive look at the Galápagos’ protected marine and volcanic landscapes. Vins said the film aims to inspire care for wildlife, and Chris M. Williams, founder and CEO of Pocket.watch, highlighted the opportunity to translate creator‑community relationships into feature‑film success.

Pocket.watch indicated that more creator‑led feature deals are in development, positioning the studio as a bridge between online personalities and traditional film distribution channels.

## Why it matters
By leveraging a creator’s built‑in fanbase, Pocket.watch reduces the marketing risk that typically accompanies independent feature releases. The strategy could reshape distribution models, giving niche content—like wildlife expeditions—broader access without relying on theatrical windows. For streaming platforms, the deal adds premium documentary content that can attract both education‑focused viewers and fans of the creator’s existing channels.

## How to think about it
If you’re a streaming watcher, treat ‘Reef To Ridge’ as a hybrid experience: a documentary with the production values of a feature film, but marketed through a creator’s community. Look for the film on YouTube first, then keep an eye on announcements from major SVOD services that may pick it up. When evaluating future creator‑led features, consider the creator’s audience size, the uniqueness of the subject matter, and the distribution plan beyond the initial platform.

<Callout type="warning">Don’t assume the film will automatically reach mainstream audiences; its success still depends on broader platform acquisition and marketing beyond the creator’s channel.</Callout>

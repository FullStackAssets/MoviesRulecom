Chipotle Mexican Grill has rolled out a limited‑edition “Sneak It In” hoodie, timed for the upcoming blockbuster releases. The sweatshirt hides an insulated pocket sized for a burrito, bowl, salad or tacos, and ships on November 18 for $53 with free U.S. and Canada shipping. With three Chipotle‑inspired colorways, the hoodie also includes a napkin pocket and a free entrée card. For movie lovers who habitually sneak snacks, the product turns a covert snack into a fashion statement. It highlights the growing crossover between food brands and cinema culture.

<Callout type="takeaway">The hoodie lets you bring a warm Chipotle burrito to the theater without breaking the rules.</Callout>

## What happened
Chipotle announced the “Sneak It In” hoodie on Nov 16, noting that 63 % of Americans have smuggled food into a movie theater, according to YouGov. The hoodie’s hidden insulated pocket is engineered to keep a standard Chipotle burrito warm until showtime, while a separate napkin pocket addresses the practical need for wipes. It will be sold exclusively at ChipotleGoods.com in the United States and Canada, with free shipping and a limited‑run of three colorways: Nothing To See Here Black, No Burrito Here Brown, and Got the Goods Gray.

Each hoodie retails for $53 and ships on November 18 at 9 a.m. PT. Purchasers receive a complimentary entrée card that can be redeemed for a burrito or other menu item, reinforcing the product’s “dinner‑and‑a‑movie” concept. Chipotle Rewards members can also enter a giveaway for a free hoodie through the Rewards Exchange.

## Why it matters
The launch taps into the long‑standing habit of moviegoers bringing their own food, a practice that theater chains have tried to curb for decades. By offering a stylish, brand‑aligned solution, Chipotle turns a rule‑bending behavior into a marketing opportunity, potentially shifting consumer expectations around theater concessions. The product also signals how food brands are leveraging pop‑culture moments to deepen engagement with younger, on‑the‑go audiences.

## How to think about it
If you’re planning a movie night and want to avoid concession prices, check your theater’s outside‑food policy before wearing the hoodie. Use the insulated pocket for a single burrito or salad to stay within size limits, and keep the napkin pocket handy for clean‑up. Consider the hoodie as a dual‑purpose item: a fashion piece for everyday wear and a niche tool for movie outings, weighing the $53 cost against the convenience of skipping pricey theater snacks.

<Callout type="warning">Many theaters prohibit outside food, so using the hoodie may violate house rules and result in being asked to leave.</Callout>

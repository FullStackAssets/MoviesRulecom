Disney+ and Hulu are set to launch the long‑awaited Season 3 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians this Friday, marking the first on‑screen adaptation of Rick Riordan’s The Titan’s Curse. The eight‑part arc expands the series to 24 episodes across three seasons, cementing its place as the biggest fantasy release of the weekend. With a near‑perfect 97% Rotten Tomatoes score and a perfect 100% rating for the new season, the show is poised to dominate streaming charts. Fans of the books will finally see the story they’ve been waiting for, while new viewers can dive into a fresh adventure. The launch underscores Disney+’s strategy to revive the franchise after the underwhelming film adaptations.
<Callout type="takeaway">Season 3’s premiere of The Titan’s Curse is Disney+’s most significant fantasy event this weekend, promising high viewership and critical acclaim.</Callout>
## What happened
The series returns on Friday, November 20, with a two‑part premiere that will kick off the eighth episode of the show. The new season adapts the third book in Riordan’s series, a story that had not previously been filmed. Disney+ and Hulu are the exclusive streaming platforms for the release.
Season 1 drew 13.3 million viewers in its first six days, establishing the show as a top‑tier streaming hit. The series now boasts a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score, with the new season receiving a perfect 100% rating from critics. Rick Riordan, who serves as an executive producer, has praised the adaptation for staying true to the source material.
## Why it matters
The premiere signals Disney+’s commitment to expanding its original fantasy catalog and capitalizing on the enduring popularity of the Percy Jackson brand. By delivering the first on‑screen adaptation of The Titan’s Curse, the studio addresses long‑time fans’ disappointment with the earlier film series. The high Rotten Tomatoes score and strong early viewership suggest that the show could become a benchmark for future book‑to‑screen projects, influencing how streaming platforms approach adaptations.

Liam Neeson, the longtime voice of Aslan, was caught off‑guard when he learned that Meryl Streep will assume the iconic lion’s role in Greta Gerwig’s upcoming Narnia adaptation. In a brief interview with ScreenRant, Neeson admitted, “I’m sorry, this is news to me,” before adding a wry endorsement of Streep’s suitability. Gerwig, who is directing and writing the film, confirmed that Streep’s gravitas and sense of wonder made her the top choice. The casting marks a generational hand‑off as the new movie, Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew, heads to Netflix theaters on Feb. 12. Fans and industry watchers are watching closely to see how this shift will reshape the beloved series.

## What happened
Liam Neeson, who voiced Aslan in the 2005‑2010 Disney‑Warner Bros. trilogy, reacted to the news that Meryl Streep would voice the character in Gerwig’s adaptation. He told a ScreenRant reporter, “Meryl is playing Jesus Christ? Well, if you’re going to make Christ a lady, nobody better than Meryl.”

Gerwig explained her casting criteria in an interview with Empire, noting she wanted someone “profound but not pretentious, with gravitas but not morose self‑seriousness.” Streep, now over 70, fits that description, offering both wisdom and child‑like wonder.

The project adapts C.S. Lewis’s sixth book, The Magician’s Nephew, which chronicles Aslan’s creation of Narnia. Netflix confirmed the film will debut in theaters on Feb. 12, 2027, after a 2023 announcement of Gerwig’s involvement.

## Why it matters
The hand‑off from Neeson to Streep underscores a broader shift toward more diverse and gender‑fluid casting in high‑profile franchises. Streep’s involvement may attract an older, awards‑oriented audience while preserving the mythic weight of Aslan for younger viewers. Industry insiders see the move as a vote of confidence in Gerwig’s vision, potentially influencing future streaming‑first adaptations of classic literature.

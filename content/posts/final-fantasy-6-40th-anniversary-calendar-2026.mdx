Square Enix has confirmed that a Final Fantasy 2027 calendar will hit stores in November 2026, marking the franchise’s 40th anniversary. The calendar spotlights Final Fantasy 6 with a striking Terra Branford illustration and will be available for pre‑order in Japan at ¥4,950. At the same time, the company teased a new spin‑off titled Final Fantasy Resonance, built on the Brave Exvius platform and featuring characters like Cloud and Terra. These moves give longtime fans fresh collectibles while signaling Square Enix’s strategy to celebrate the past without a new core entry. The announcements set the tone for the upcoming anniversary year.

<Callout type="takeaway">The November 2026 calendar and Resonance spin‑off together anchor the 40th‑anniversary celebrations.</Callout>

## What happened
Square Enix announced that a Final Fantasy 2027 calendar will be released on November 19, 2026, with pre‑orders currently open only in Japan for ¥4,950. The A4‑size calendar features artwork for the first quarter of 2027, beginning with a Terra Branford scene that references her original Yoshitaka Amano concept art.

In parallel, the studio revealed that work is underway on Final Fantasy Resonance, a spin‑off built on the Brave Exvius engine. The title will blend gameplay from the mobile game with a premium skin and include crossover heroes such as Cloud Strife from Final Fantasy 7 and Terra Branford from Final Fantasy 6.

## Why it matters
The calendar serves as a physical reminder of the series’ legacy, giving collectors a reason to engage during the anniversary year while the spin‑off expands the franchise into the mobile space. By limiting the pre‑order to Japan, Square Enix creates scarcity that may drive demand, but it also risks alienating international fans eager for new content. The dual focus on nostalgia and new digital experiences illustrates how the company balances heritage with future growth.

Treat the calendar as a limited‑edition collectible and decide if the price aligns with your collecting budget.

November 2026 is shaping up to be a landmark month for Prime Video, with a mix of high‑profile series returns, fresh sci‑fi sequels, and a wave of holiday programming. The platform will debut season 3 of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power and launch Blade Runner 2099, two titles that dominate the streaming conversation. At the same time, Amazon is bolstering its sports slate—NFL, NBA, soccer and golf—while dusting off classic Christmas movies for the season’s festivities.

<Callout type="takeaway">Prime Video’s November slate balances blockbuster series, genre sequels, sports, and holiday classics to capture every viewer’s interest.</Callout>

## What happened
Prime Video is rolling out season 3 of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, continuing the high‑budget fantasy saga that has anchored the service’s original lineup. Alongside the fantasy flagship, the sci‑fi sequel Blade Runner 2099 arrives, promising to extend the iconic Blade Runner universe for a new generation. The month also sees the re‑release of classic films such as the 1999 version of A Christmas Carol starring Patrick Stewart, the family adventure Agent Cody Banks 2: Destination London, and the holiday short O’ Christmas Tree.

In addition to scripted content, Prime Video is expanding its live‑sports offering, streaming NFL, NBA, soccer, and golf events throughout November. The platform is also refreshing its holiday catalog with a broader selection of Christmas classics, giving subscribers seasonal options alongside the new releases.

## Why it matters
The convergence of flagship series, genre sequels, and seasonal programming positions Prime Video to attract a diverse audience during a competitive streaming period. High‑profile titles like The Rings of Power and Blade Runner 2099 generate buzz that can drive subscriber growth, while sports streams and holiday movies keep existing members engaged. By layering new content with familiar classics, Amazon aims to maximize viewership across demographics and capitalize on the holiday viewing surge.

## How to think about it
Treat November’s lineup as a curated mix: prioritize the flagship series if you’re a fan of long‑form storytelling, slot the Blade Runner sequel for a sci‑fi fix, and use the sports schedule to fill evenings with live events.

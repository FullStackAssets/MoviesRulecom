The week of November 2-8 marked a significant downturn for original streaming content, with total viewing minutes for the top ten originals dropping by 21 percent. Despite this soft performance, the sci-fi horror series From continued its dominance of the overall rankings, securing the number one spot for the third consecutive week. This divergence highlights a growing reliance on acquired content to drive streaming engagement while new original releases struggle to maintain momentum.

<Callout type="takeaway">Acquired content like From is currently outperforming original series, which saw a sharp 21 percent decline in weekly viewing minutes.</Callout>

## What happened
Nielsen data for the week of November 2-8 shows that From, which originates on MGM+ but is widely available on Netflix, recorded 1.69 billion minutes of viewing. This figure nearly doubled the 857 million minutes recorded by the second-place show, Grey’s Anatomy. The series has now held the top overall position for three straight weeks, demonstrating sustained viewer interest in the franchise.

In contrast, the original series chart led by Love Is Blind, which accumulated 691 million minutes of viewing. This total represents the lowest figure for the top original show in over two years, with the previous low occurring in early August 2024. The top ten original series combined for 4.95 billion minutes, a 21 percent decrease from the previous week. Other notable original performances included Nobody Wants This with 559 million minutes and The Diplomat with 545 million minutes.

The overall top ten streaming titles saw a 13 percent decline, dropping from 9.03 billion to 7.84 billion minutes. While some of this drop may be attributed to viewers following midterm election results, the decline is more pronounced than during the 2024 presidential election week, when three original series individually exceeded one billion minutes of watch time.

## Why it matters
The disparity between acquired and original content performance signals a potential shift in streaming strategy for platforms. With original series struggling to generate high engagement, studios may face pressure to invest more in proven franchises or acquired library content that retains audiences. The fact that From, a non-Netflix original, leads the overall chart underscores the importance of multi-platform distribution and the lingering appeal of specific genres like sci-fi horror.

For viewers, the surge in acquired content like The Big Bang Theory, which recorded its 53rd straight week on the charts with 817 million minutes, indicates a nostalgia-driven trend. Similarly, Bluey remains a perennial favorite with 801 million minutes, marking its 214th week on the list. The early appearance of holiday films like Home Alone and Dr. Seuss’ The Grinch, which typically debut closer to Thanksgiving, suggests platforms are front-loading seasonal content to capture early holiday viewing.

## How to think about it
When analyzing streaming performance, it is crucial to distinguish between overall rankings and original series charts. A show like From can dominate the overall landscape due to its broad appeal and multi-platform availability, while original series may struggle to compete with established franchises. Viewers and analysts should monitor whether this trend of acquired content outperforming originals persists, as it may influence future production budgets and marketing strategies. Additionally, the early debut of holiday films suggests that platforms are adjusting their release schedules to maximize seasonal engagement, a tactic worth noting for future content planning.

<Callout type="warning">Nielsen’s streaming ratings only cover viewing on TV sets and do not include minutes watched on mobile devices or tablets, meaning total viewership may be higher than reported.</Callout>

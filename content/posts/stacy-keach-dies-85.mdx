Stacy Keach, the veteran actor best known for his hard‑boiled portrayal of private eye Mike Hammer and his role in the cult classic Fat City, died on November 10, 2026 at age 85. The cause was heart failure, confirmed by his wife Malgosia in a statement released from their family home in Magdalenka, Poland. Keach’s death marks the end of a six‑decade career that spanned film, television, and stage, influencing generations of performers. Fans and industry peers alike are reflecting on his distinctive voice and commanding presence.

## What happened
Stacy Keach died of heart failure at his residence in Magdalenka, Poland, as confirmed by his wife Malgosia in a family‑issued statement. The announcement noted a long battle with a serious heart condition that ultimately led to his peaceful departure.

The actor is survived by his wife, their children Karolina and Shannon, and grandchildren. Keach’s career included the iconic TV role of Mike Hammer, a memorable turn in the 1972 film Fat City, and numerous stage performances that earned him critical acclaim.

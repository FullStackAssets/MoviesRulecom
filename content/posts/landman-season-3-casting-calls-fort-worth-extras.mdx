The hit streaming drama Landman has opened its third season casting doors to everyday Fort Worth residents. Production announced a new round of extra positions, inviting locals to appear on set in a variety of background roles. The call was posted on the Dallas News website and shared across social media channels. For fans and aspiring performers, this represents a rare chance to be part of a high‑profile series without a formal acting résumé.

<Callout type="takeaway">Landman's season 3 extra casting offers Fort Worth locals a direct path onto a major streaming production.</Callout>

## What happened
The casting notice, released on October 30, 2026, specifies that the show is seeking adults and teens to fill background spots in scenes set in oil‑rich Texas towns. Applicants are instructed to submit a brief video introduction, a recent photo, and availability dates through the provided online portal, with callbacks scheduled over the next few weeks.

## Why it matters
Bringing local extras into a high‑budget series not only adds authenticity to the on‑screen world but also creates a pipeline for regional talent to gain industry exposure, potentially feeding future casting decisions for larger roles.

## How to think about it
Treat the extra application as a stepping stone: polish your short video, be punctual for any callbacks, and use the experience to build a reel or résumé. Even without a speaking part, the exposure to set etiquette and crew workflow can be valuable for future auditions.

<Callout type="warning">Do not assume a role will lead to a speaking part; most extra positions remain non‑credited background work.</Callout>

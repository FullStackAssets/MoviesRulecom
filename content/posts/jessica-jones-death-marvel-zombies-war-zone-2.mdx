Marvel Zombies: War Zone #2 delivered a gut‑punch for fans when Jessica Jones was brutally killed by a trusted ally. The issue reveals that the Hand ninjas’ outbreak on Manhattan’s streets turned Jess into one of the first victims, and Frank Castle’s intervention sealed her fate. This shocking twist not only deepens the horror of the series but also reshapes the emotional stakes for the surviving heroes.

<Callout type="takeaway">Jessica Jones’s death marks the series’ darkest turning point.</Callout>

## What happened
Jessica Jones is bitten by a zombified Hand ninja during the early chaos on Manhattan’s island, a moment captured in a flashback from Luke Cage’s perspective. The bite quickly turns her into a zombie, and Frank Castle discovers her condition while tending to survivors in a makeshift infirmary.

Instead of letting her become a threat, Frank makes the grim decision to end her life, delivering a swift, fatal blow that prevents further infection. The scene is framed as a heartbreaking sacrifice, underscoring the brutal reality of the Hunger outbreak and the impossible choices the heroes must make.

## Why it matters
Jessica Jones’s death raises the emotional stakes of Marvel Zombies: War Zone, showing that even iconic, hardened characters are not safe. It forces the remaining heroes—Luke, Iron Fist, and others—to confront the loss of a teammate and to reevaluate their strategies for survival. The twist also signals to readers that the series will continue to prioritize high‑stakes horror over fan‑service nostalgia.

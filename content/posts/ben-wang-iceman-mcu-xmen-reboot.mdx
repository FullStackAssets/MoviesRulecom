The Marvel Cinematic Universe is moving closer to its long‑awaited X‑Men integration, and the latest casting buzz centers on Ben Wang potentially stepping into the role of Iceman. Deadline reported that Wang, known for his work on *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, is circling the part as the studio finalizes its ensemble. Alongside him, Sadie Sink is confirmed as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, and Indie Navarrette as Rogue. This lineup signals a fresh take on the mutant team while anchoring it with recognizable talent. For fans tracking the MCU’s expansion, the news reshapes expectations for how the reboot will blend with existing storylines.

## What happened
Deadline’s source confirmed that Ben Wang is in active negotiations to portray Iceman in Marvel Studios’ upcoming X‑Men reboot. The report placed Wang among a growing list of confirmed actors, including Sadie Sink (Jean Grey), Kit Connor (Cyclops), Samara Weaving (Emma Frost), and Indie Navarrette (Rogue). While no official contract has been disclosed, the inclusion of Wang suggests the studio is prioritizing both fresh faces and actors with genre experience.

The broader casting announcement came as Marvel continues to map the X‑Men into the post‑Infinity Saga timeline. By aligning the new team with characters already introduced in the MCU, Marvel aims to create narrative continuity while delivering a distinct mutant storyline.

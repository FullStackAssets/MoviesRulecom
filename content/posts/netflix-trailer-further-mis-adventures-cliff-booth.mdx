Netflix just dropped the first official trailer for David Fincher’s upcoming film The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. The teaser reunites audiences with Brad Pitt’s iconic character, the rugged stuntman who first appeared in the 2019 hit Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. With its gritty tone and hints of new conspiracies, the clip signals a darker turn for the franchise and sets expectations for the streaming giant’s next big original.

<Callout type="takeaway">The new trailer confirms that Brad Pitt’s Cliff Booth is heading into a more shadowy, mystery‑driven sequel on Netflix.</Callout>

## What happened
On November 6, 2026, Netflix and director David Fincher released a 2‑minute trailer for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. The clip opens with a smoky desert landscape before cutting to Booth (Pitt) navigating a tense standoff in a dimly lit bar.

Brief flashes show a new supporting cast, a vintage car chase, and a cryptic symbol that suggests a hidden organization pulling strings behind Hollywood’s glitz.

The trailer ends with the tagline “Every legend has a secret,” and confirms a 2027 release window on Netflix.

## Why it matters
Fincher’s involvement elevates the project from a nostalgic spin‑off to a potential auteur‑driven thriller, while Pitt’s return provides a built‑in audience eager for continuity. For Netflix, the trailer serves as a high‑stakes marketing hook to attract both cinephiles and casual subscribers ahead of a competitive 2027 slate. The darker tone hints at a shift in the franchise’s narrative focus, which could redefine how legacy characters are leveraged in streaming originals.

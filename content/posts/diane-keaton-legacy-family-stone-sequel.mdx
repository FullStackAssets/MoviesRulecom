The original 2005 holiday film "The Family Stone" returns with a sequel titled "The Families Stones". Dermot Mulroney, who played Everett Stone, confirmed at a recent premiere that the project is designed to honor Diane Keaton’s memory. The sequel follows the Stone family 20 years after their mother’s passing, reuniting for Christmas. Fans are watching closely because the original remains a beloved seasonal staple. The production also adds new talent such as Jean Smart and Claire Danes.

<Callout type="takeaway">The sequel is positioned as a heartfelt tribute to Diane Keaton rather than a standard franchise reboot.</Callout>

## What happened
Mulroney told Variety at the "The Hunting Wives" Season 2 premiere that “Diane’s memory is honored in everything.” He suggested the film has “a different quality than just your garden variety sequel,” crediting the family’s reunion for its resonance.

Titled “The Families Stones,” the story tracks the Stone siblings two decades after their mother’s death and introduces a surprise when their father brings home a new love interest. Returning cast members include Sarah Jessica Parker, Rachel McAdams, Craig T. Nelson, Luke Wilson, Tyrone Giordano, Elizabeth Reaser, Paul Schneider and Brian White, while Jean Smart and Claire Danes join the ensemble.

Photos of Mulroney on set with Parker and McAdams went viral in October, sparking renewed buzz among fans of the original film.

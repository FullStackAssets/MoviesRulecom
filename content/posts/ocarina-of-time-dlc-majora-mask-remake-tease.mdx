A newly uncovered piece of Ocarina of Time DLC data has set the Zelda community abuzz. The files contain a mysterious mask entry and dialogue lines that do not appear in the released game. Analysts link the mask to a potential Majora’s Mask remake and an accompanying amiibo slated for 2028. If true, Nintendo could be expanding its Switch 2 lineup with the long‑awaited sequel remake.
<Callout type="takeaway">The DLC leak strongly hints that a Majora’s Mask remake is on Nintendo’s roadmap.</Callout>
## What happened
Nintendo’s 2023 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time received universal praise and solid sales, establishing it as a flagship title for the upcoming Switch 2. Recent datamining of the game’s DLC files revealed an undocumented mask labeled 103, accompanied by NPC dialogue describing it as "weird," "distracting," and likening Link to a "monster." 
Content creator Austin John Plays connected the odd entry to the Skull Kid amiibo, suggesting the mask would unlock Majora’s Mask content when the figure is used, and that the amiibo could launch alongside a full remake of Majora’s Mask in 2028.
## Why it matters
A Majora’s Mask remake would deepen Nintendo’s strategy of revitalizing classic franchises for new hardware, potentially driving early Switch 2 adoption and boosting amiibo revenue. It also raises expectations among a passionate fan base that has waited decades for a modern version of the sequel, making the announcement a pivotal moment for the franchise’s future direction.

Treat the leak as a signal, not a confirmation. Monitor official Nintendo channels for any formal announcement, and consider the timing of a possible amiibo release before planning purchases. Weigh the excitement of a new remake against the risk that the details could change or be delayed.

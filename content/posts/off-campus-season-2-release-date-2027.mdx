Prime Video has officially confirmed the return of the college drama Off Campus for a second season. The streaming service posted on X that the new season will premiere on April 28, 2027. The announcement ties the series to Elle Kennedy’s third novel, The Score, and reunites leads Mika Abdalla and Stephen Kalyn. Fans of the romance‑drama will now have a concrete timeline for when the next chapter arrives.

## What happened
Prime Video shared a post on X confirming that Off Campus season 2 will launch on April 28, 2027. The update highlighted that the sophomore season adapts Elle Kennedy’s third novel, *The Score*, and will continue to follow the romance between Allie Hayes (Mika Abdalla) and Dean Di Laurentis (Stephen Kalyn).

The series, which debuted in 2026, centers on a college hockey team’s romantic entanglements at the fictional Briar University. By confirming the returning cast and the specific release window, Prime Video gives viewers a clear expectation for the continuation of the story.

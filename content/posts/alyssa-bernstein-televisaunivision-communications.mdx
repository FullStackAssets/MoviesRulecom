TelevisaUnivision announced Tuesday that Alyssa Bernstein has been promoted to Head of Corporate Communications. The move follows the departure of former chief communications officer Oscar Suris last August and reflects a broader restructuring of the company's messaging function. Bernstein, who joined the Hispanic media giant in 2022 and rose to senior vice president a year later, will now oversee media relations, executive communications, branding and social media. The promotion signals TelevisaUnivision’s intent to tighten its corporate narrative as it navigates divergent U.S. and Mexico market dynamics. Stakeholders are watching to see how the new leadership will shape the company’s public profile.

<Callout type="takeaway">Alyssa Bernstein’s expanded role centralizes TelevisaUnivision’s messaging under a single, business‑focused leader.</Callout>

## What happened
TelevisaUnivision confirmed that Bernstein will report directly to chief human resources officer Karina Awad and will continue close collaboration with CEO Daniel Alegre. Her responsibilities now include corporate branding, social media, and integrated communications for both U.S. and Mexican operations.

Bernstein’s tenure at the company began after the 2022 merger between Univision and Grupo Televisa’s media arm. Prior to joining, she held communications positions at Paramount, CBS and ABC News, giving her a broad entertainment‑industry perspective.

The promotion comes as TelevisaUnivision reports strong advertising revenue in Mexico, partly driven by its FIFA Men’s World Cup rights, while maintaining quarterly earnings calls with Wall Street analysts.

## Why it matters
Consolidating communications under a seasoned executive signals a strategic push to present a unified brand narrative across two distinct markets. A cohesive message can bolster advertiser confidence and improve the company’s positioning amid competitive streaming and broadcast landscapes. However, the effectiveness of this change will depend on how well the new structure aligns with broader business objectives and regional nuances.

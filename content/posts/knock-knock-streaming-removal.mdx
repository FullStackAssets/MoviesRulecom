## What happened
The movie was quietly removed from every major on‑demand service as of early November 2026, with no official statement from the platforms explaining the timing. Industry monitors noted the disappearance coincided with a broader purge of older R‑rated thrillers that generate limited viewership. The removal affects both subscription services and ad‑supported platforms that previously offered the film as part of their catalog.

The film, directed by Eli Roth, follows architect Evan Webber (Keanu Reeves) whose act of kindness to two stranded women spirals into a sexually charged, violent nightmare. Despite its provocative premise, the movie never achieved commercial success, earning only $6.3 million at the box office.

Finally, the pull highlights how licensing agreements and content performance metrics can abruptly erase a film from public access.

Its arrival on a free platform highlights how streaming services are reshaping access to controversial yet influential cinema. ## What happened
In November 2026 Pluto added Django Unchained to its ad‑supported catalog, making the 2012 Tarantino western available at no cost.
The film, which blends the stylized violence of Kill Bill with the dusty visual language of Sergio Leone’s spaghetti westerns, was originally praised for its bold re‑imagining of the 1966 Django character.
Tarantino has long cited Leone’s The Good, the Bad and the Ugly as a key influence, and the movie’s soundtrack and framing echo that tradition while inserting his signature dialogue rhythms.

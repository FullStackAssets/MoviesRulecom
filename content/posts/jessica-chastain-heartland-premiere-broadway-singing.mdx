Jessica Chastain stepped onto the red carpet in New York City on November 5 to premiere Netflix’s new country‑music mystery, Heartland. The film follows former star Misty Jones as she investigates her niece’s disappearance amid Nashville’s dark underbelly. Alongside co‑stars Garrett Hedlund, Carter Faith, Ross Lynch, Ben Dickey, Paul Sparks, Skyler Day, and Sarah Helbringer, Chastain highlighted the project’s blend of suspense and music. In a separate interview, she clarified why she has no plans to sing on Broadway. The debut and her comments are shaping expectations for the film’s streaming launch on November 13.

<Callout type="takeaway">Heartland showcases Chastain’s dramatic range while confirming she will stay out of live musical theater.</Callout>

## What happened
At the Manhattan premiere, Chastain arrived with co‑star Garrett Hedlund and director‑writer Shana Feste. The event featured the full cast, including Carter Faith, Ross Lynch, Ben Dickey, Paul Sparks, Skyler Day, and Sarah Helbringer, who posed for photos and answered questions about the film’s tone.

The story centers on former country megastar Misty Jones, played by Chastain, who emerges from seclusion to investigate her missing niece, a rising musician. Her search uncovers a murky side of Nashville where loyalties shift and no one is who they appear to be.

During an appearance on Watch What Happens Live, Chastain explained she lacks the training to deliver consistent live performances, contrasting her work with singer‑actress Jennifer Nettles, and therefore will not pursue Broadway singing.

## Why it matters
Heartland’s blend of mystery and country music expands Netflix’s genre portfolio, appealing to both thriller fans and music lovers. Chastain’s involvement adds prestige, potentially drawing viewers who might not normally watch a country‑themed story. Her public dismissal of Broadway singing also manages fan expectations, preventing speculation that could distract from the film’s narrative.

Marvel had already plotted Doctor Doom’s MCU debut before Jonathan Majors was fired, reshaping the Avengers saga. The original plan for Avengers: The Kang Dynasty, featuring Majors as Kang the Conqueror, was upended after his arrest and conviction. Marvel quickly pivoted, replacing the title with Avengers: Doomsday and bringing in Robert Downey Jr. as the villainous Doctor Doom. Kevin Feige revealed that the Doom storyline was in the works even before the Kang project fell apart. This shift signals a major narrative reset for the MCU’s multiverse arc.

## What happened

In July 2022, Kevin Feige announced Avengers: The Kang Dynasty at Comic-Con, positioning Jonathan Majors as the next big MCU villain. The film was set to launch the multiverse storyline starting with 2023’s Ant‑Man and the Wasp Quantumania.

After Quantumania’s lukewarm reception and Majors’ March 2023 arrest for assault, Marvel terminated his contract in December 2023 following his conviction. The studio then announced a new title, Avengers: Doomsday, and a pivot to Doctor Doom as the central antagonist.

Feige said the Doom concept had been discussed long before Majors’ firing, and the Kang Dynasty was originally intended as a bait‑and‑switch leading to Doom’s reveal.

## Why it matters

The rewrite alters the MCU’s villain hierarchy, replacing a new character with a classic comic foe. It also reflects Marvel’s swift response to legal and public‑relations crises, ensuring the franchise stays on schedule. Fans now face a different narrative trajectory, with implications for future films and the multiverse structure.

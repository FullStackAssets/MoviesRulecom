The DC Universe got a surprise on Nov. 9, 2026 when Warner Bros. Discovery announced that James Gunn and Peter Safran are stepping down as heads of DC Studios. Their departure ends a four‑year run that launched six movies and shows between 2024 and 2026. The shake‑up arrives just as the studio prepares its next wave of films, making the leadership change a pivotal moment for the franchise.

<Callout type="takeaway">James Gunn and Peter Safran’s exit signals a major strategic shift for the DCU.</Callout>

## What happened
Warner Bros. Discovery confirmed on its quarterly earnings call that Gunn and Safran will leave DC Studios effective Dec. 1, 2026. The duo had been hired in October 2022 by then‑CEO David Zaslav to launch the new DC Universe, delivering six live‑action and animated projects from 2024‑2026.

Skydance announced that veteran producer Jeremy Latcham will replace them. Latcham, who rose to senior vice‑president of Production and Development at Marvel Studios, produced Iron Man, The Avengers, Guardians of the Galaxy, Avengers: Age of Ultron and Spider‑Man: Homecoming, accumulating over $5 billion in box‑office revenue. He will report to Skydance Motion Picture Group co‑chairs Dana Goldberg and Josh Greenstein.

## Why it matters
The leadership turnover could reshape the tonal direction, marketing strategy, and cross‑media integration of the DCU. Fans and investors are watching to see whether Latcham will import Marvel‑style continuity planning or forge a distinct path, while existing projects must navigate a transition without losing momentum.

## How to think about it
Treat the change as a strategic pivot rather than a complete reboot. Keep an eye on upcoming release calendars, watch for statements from Latcham about tone and continuity, and consider how existing story arcs might be re‑framed under new oversight.

<Callout type="warning">Do not assume that all upcoming DC projects will be scrapped; many are already deep in production and will likely proceed under the new leadership.</Callout>

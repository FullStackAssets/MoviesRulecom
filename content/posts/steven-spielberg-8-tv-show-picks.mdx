## What happened
Steven Spielberg disclosed a curated list of eight television programs he has been watching, during a recent conversation with Screen Rant writer Cher Thompson. Among the titles, the Canadian sketch comedy series SCTV (Second City Television) received particular emphasis. SCTV originally aired from 1976 to 1981 and launched the careers of comedians such as Catherine O’Hara, John Candy, Eugene Levy, Martin Short, Rick Moranis, and Andrea Martin. The series is praised for its inventive approach to sketch comedy, a quality that resonates with Spielberg’s appreciation for creative storytelling.

The broader list spans several decades and genres, reflecting Spielberg’s willingness to explore both nostalgic classics and modern narratives. While the complete lineup is detailed in the source article, the inclusion of SCTV signals a recognition of how early television comedy can influence contemporary visual storytelling.

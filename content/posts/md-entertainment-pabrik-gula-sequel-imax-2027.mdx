MD Entertainment unveiled its upcoming slate at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest, confirming a sequel to the 2025 horror sensation “Pabrik Gula.” The sequel is slated for early 2028, while the studio also announced three IMAX releases slated for 2027. The announcements follow the film’s domestic success of over 5 million tickets and its expansion into Vietnam, Cambodia, Singapore, Malaysia, Brunei, the U.S. and Europe. MD’s broader strategy includes leveraging its recent $105 million acquisition of NET TV and partnerships with Lionsgate and CJ.

<Callout type="takeaway">MD is using the “Pabrik Gula” brand to anchor an aggressive IMAX and sequel push across Southeast Asia.</Callout>

## What happened
MD Entertainment said the “Pabrik Gula” sequel will arrive in early 2028, capitalising on the original’s more than 5 million‑ticket run and strong regional fanbase. At the same event the company outlined three IMAX titles for 2027, one of which will adapt the YouTube‑hit “Dhot Design,” a series that draws between 8 million and 10 million views per episode. The IMAX effort builds on the earlier success of “Badarawuhi,” the first Southeast Asian film shot for the format.

Beyond horror, MD plans 12 to 13 releases in 2027 across comedy, drama and animation, adding to a catalog of over 200 features. Recent acquisitions include control of free‑to‑air NET TV in a deal valued up to $105 million, and new equity from Korea’s SBS, positioning the studio for broader distribution and streaming deals.

## Why it matters
The sequel and IMAX slate signal MD’s intent to dominate the regional premium‑format market, a space traditionally led by Hollywood studios. By pairing a proven horror franchise with high‑budget IMAX productions, MD can command higher ticket prices and attract international partners such as Lionsgate and CJ. Success will likely influence other Southeast Asian studios to pursue large‑scale formats and cross‑border releases, reshaping the region’s cinema economics.

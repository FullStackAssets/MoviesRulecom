## What happened
Collider published a feature titled “Only 3 Movies Are More Entertaining Than The Lord of the Rings.” The piece highlights three titles that, in the author’s view, deliver more nonstop excitement than the three‑film Tolkien adaptation. The first film discussed is Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), praised for its relentless action, charismatic lead, and emotionally grounded stakes. The article briefly outlines the other two selections, noting that each balances high‑octane set pieces with clear character motivations, allowing audiences to become instantly invested.
The write‑up emphasizes how each movie respects its characters’ choices even amid chaos, ensuring that victories feel earned and sacrifices linger. By focusing on human emotion rather than sheer spectacle, the list argues these films keep viewers “hooked” from start to finish.

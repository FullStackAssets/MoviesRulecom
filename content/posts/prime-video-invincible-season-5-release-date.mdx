Fans of the adult‑animated superhero series have a new date to mark on their calendars. Prime Video confirmed that Invincible Season 5 will debut on February 17, 2027, and released a fresh teaser set in the series’ iconic fast‑food chain, Burger Mart. The announcement arrives after Season 4 became the most‑watched season in the show’s history, raising expectations for the next chapter. With the series’ star‑studded voice cast returning, the teaser hints at the moral dilemmas that will define the new episodes.
<Callout type="takeaway">Invincible Season 5 arrives on Feb. 17, 2027, with a Burger Mart‑themed teaser that teases the series’ next moral crossroads.</Callout>
## What happened
Prime Video disclosed the official release date for Invincible Season 5 as February 17, 2027, and simultaneously dropped a 30‑second teaser that centers on the fictional fast‑food chain Burger Mart, a recurring setting in the series. The season will continue the story of Mark Grayson after the “earth‑shattering events” of Season 4, following his quest to decide what kind of hero he must become. The show retains its core voice talent—including Steven Yeun, Sandra Oh, J.K. Simmons, and a supporting cast that features Jonathan Banks, Mark Hamill, and Seth Rogen—under the production of Skybound Animation. Executive producers Robert Kirkman, David Alpert, Simon Racioppa, Margaret M. Dean, Catherine Winder, Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg confirmed that the fifth season will explore higher stakes as millions of lives hang in the balance.

The film press got an early look at David Fincher’s Netflix‑backed sequel, The Further Mis‑Adventures of Cliff Booth, and the response has been overwhelmingly positive. Critics describe the movie as a wild, entertaining love letter to 1970s Hollywood, blending Tarantino’s chaos with Fincher’s precision. The buzz positions the sequel as one of the year’s most anticipated releases, especially for fans of the original Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. Its early acclaim could shape streaming strategies and set expectations for future franchise extensions.

<Callout type="takeaway">The sequel is being celebrated as a wildly entertaining love letter that lives up to its iconic predecessor.</Callout>

## What happened
David Fincher, Quentin Tarantino and Brad Pitt unveiled the sequel, officially titled The Further Mis‑Adventures of Cliff Booth, at a press screening organized for film journalists.

Critics such as Simon Thompson praised its 'pop, throbs and crackles,' while Zach Pope highlighted the blend of Tarantino’s chaos, Fincher’s detail, and Pitt’s charisma. Drew Taylor called it 'an absolute triumph,' and other reviewers echoed the sentiment, labeling it a 'wildly entertaining hangout movie.'

The reaction has already placed the film among the year’s best‑received titles, with early buzz suggesting a strong subscriber draw for Netflix.

## Why it matters
The enthusiastic press response signals that the sequel could become a key driver of Netflix’s subscriber growth, especially among cinephiles who value auteur collaborations. By marrying Tarantino’s signature chaos with Fincher’s meticulous style, the film sets a precedent for high‑budget streaming originals that aim to rival theatrical releases. Its success may also encourage studios to revisit beloved side characters, expanding franchise universes beyond the original leads.

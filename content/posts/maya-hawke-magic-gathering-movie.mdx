Maya Hawke, known for her breakout role in Netflix’s Stranger Things, is set to join the upcoming live‑action Magic: The Gathering film. The project, a collaboration between Legendary Entertainment and Hasbro Entertainment, will bring the iconic trading‑card universe to the big screen. Hawke’s involvement marks her first major post‑Stranger Things role and signals the studio’s push for star power in the franchise. Fans of the game and the actress alike are eager to see how the multiverse of Magic will translate to cinema. The announcement also highlights the growing trend of adapting tabletop IPs into blockbuster movies.

<Callout type="takeaway">Maya Hawke’s casting gives the Magic: The Gathering movie a high‑profile anchor as it moves toward production.</Callout>

## What happened
Legendary Entertainment and Hasbro Entertainment confirmed that a live‑action feature film based on Magic: The Gathering is in development, with Matt Johnson attached to direct and co‑write alongside Noah Gardner and Aidan Fitzgerald. Sources reported that Maya Hawke has accepted a key, though undisclosed, role, becoming the first major star offered a part in the ensemble cast.

The film aims to launch a broader cinematic and television universe for the billion‑dollar brand, which now spans digital games, streaming content, and cross‑media collaborations. Production details remain under wraps, but the partnership signals a significant investment in adapting the game’s rich lore for mainstream audiences.

## Why it matters
Magic: The Gathering boasts over 50 million fans worldwide, and a successful film could expand its cultural footprint and drive new players to the game. Hawke’s involvement brings star appeal that may attract viewers beyond the existing fanbase, potentially boosting box‑office prospects and ancillary revenue. Moreover, the project reflects the industry’s shift toward leveraging established gaming franchises to fill the blockbuster pipeline.

Marvel’s next big supernatural rider is taking shape as rumors confirm a new co‑star. Connor Storrie, the breakout actor from the gay‑romantic hockey series Heated Rivalry, is reportedly in talks to appear opposite Ryan Gosling in the upcoming Ghost Rider film. The project, directed by Shawn Levy and slated for a July 28, 2028 release, will begin principal photography in New York next year. If the casting moves forward, Storrie would transition from indie acclaim to a marquee MCU role, a leap that could reshape his career trajectory.

## What happened
Connor Storrie, who supported himself as a waiter before breaking out on the series Heated Rivalry, is in advanced discussions to join Ryan Gosling in Marvel Studios' Ghost Rider. The film reunites director Shawn Levy with Gosling and screenwriter Jonathan Tropper, and Linus Sandgren has been hired as director of photography, with shooting planned on film in New York next year.

Marvel announced the project at San Diego Comic‑Con, confirming a July 28, 2028 release date. While Gosling is presumed to play Johnny Blaze, Marvel has not disclosed which character Storrie might portray, though the franchise’s history of demonic antagonists fuels speculation.

Disney+ is shaping November 2026 into a must‑watch month for families and holiday lovers alike. The service rolled out two high‑profile titles—Toy Story 5 and a live‑action version of Moana—while keeping its deep catalog of classics at the ready. In total, eight standout movies, from the spooky charm of The Nightmare Before Christmas to the timeless comedy of Home Alone, are highlighted for the month. Understanding which titles deliver the biggest payoff helps subscribers plan their binge sessions and get ahead of the holiday rush.

## What happened
Disney+ added Toy Story 5, directed by Andrew Stanton, featuring returning voices and a new character Lilypad, exploring modern tech's impact on toys. The platform also introduced a live‑action adaptation of Moana, expanding its recent trend of reimagining animated hits for the big screen. Beyond premieres, Disney+ highlighted eight movies—Toy Story 5, live‑action Moana, The Nightmare Before Christmas, Home Alone, plus several superhero and fantasy titles—available to U.S. subscribers throughout November.

## Why it matters
The combination of fresh releases and holiday staples strengthens Disney+’s position against competitors like Netflix, which focuses on series and specials at the start of the month. By offering family‑friendly blockbusters and seasonal fare, Disney+ can attract both nostalgic adults and younger viewers, driving higher engagement and subscription retention during a key advertising period. Additionally, early access to new titles gives the platform a cultural talking point that can boost word‑of‑mouth promotion ahead of the holiday shopping season.

## How to think about it
Start with the new releases—Toy Story 5 and live‑action Moana—to satisfy curiosity, then schedule the holiday movies for weekend evenings, using the built‑in Disney+ watchlist to queue titles in order of mood (adventure, fantasy, comedy, holiday). Pair family viewing with themed snacks to enhance the experience, and keep an eye on Disney+’s “Coming Soon” tab for any surprise additions later in the month.

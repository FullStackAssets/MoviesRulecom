The entertainment world said goodbye to one of its most enduring talents on Oct. 8.
Betty McGuire, a centenarian who graced Broadway stages and played the beloved mother Kate Malone on the ABC sitcom Growing Pains, died of natural causes at the Motion Picture & Television Country House and Hospital.
Her career spanned more than seven decades, from original 1950s Broadway productions to guest spots on iconic TV dramas.
McGuire’s passing reminds fans of the lasting impact of character actors who bridge theater and television.

## What happened
Betty McGuire died on Oct. 8, 2026, at the Motion Picture & Television Country House and Hospital in Woodland Hills, California, from natural causes, according to a close family friend.
She was best known to TV audiences as Kate Malone, the mother‑grandmother figure on Growing Pains, appearing in 12 episodes between 1986 and 1991.
Beyond television, McGuire earned three Broadway credits in the mid‑1950s—Plain and Fancy, Happy Hunting, and Oh, Captain!—and later headlined as Mame in Las Vegas, touring productions of Gypsy, Oliver! and The Ziegfeld Follies, and numerous guest roles on shows such as Columbo, Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, and ER.

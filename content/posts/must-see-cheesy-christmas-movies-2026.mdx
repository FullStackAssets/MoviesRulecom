## What happened
The 12 Dates of Christmas lands on Disney+ on November 6, 2026, starring Lucy Hale as Christina, a woman navigating a series of twelve dates in search of a Christmas‑worthy partner. The film also features Paul Ben‑Victor and Penelope Ann Miller, and leans into modern dating anxieties while delivering the classic holiday rom‑com formula.

Hallmark’s My Christmas Cowboy arrives later in the season, pairing Amy Acker as small‑town news producer Meg Marshall with Luke Mcfarlane’s Billy Kane, a famous Hollywood presenter returning home. Their rekindled romance unfolds against a backdrop of small‑town charm and festive décor, embodying the network’s signature feel‑good holiday vibe.

Beyond Disney+ and Hallmark, Netflix and other streaming services are adding their own festive offerings, ensuring that every viewer can find a cheesy, comforting movie to match their holiday mood.

Fans of a galaxy far, far away have a reason to celebrate as Lucasfilm and Disney roll out a year‑long program marking Star Wars’ 50th anniversary in 2027. The kickoff includes a new documentary by R.J. Cutler, a theatrical re‑release of the original 1977 film on February 19, and a slate of limited‑edition collectibles. Announcements also preview a new Star Wars film, Starfighter, arriving in May, and a museum exhibition with tickets now on sale. These events tie together nostalgia and fresh content, reinforcing the franchise’s cultural staying power. The rollout signals that the saga will remain a centerpiece of Disney’s streaming and theme‑park strategies.

<Callout type="takeaway">The 50th‑anniversary campaign blends new storytelling with iconic releases to keep Star Wars at the forefront of pop culture.</Callout>

## What happened
Lucasfilm and Disney revealed a documentary directed by R.J. Cutler that delves into five decades of Star Wars lore, set to debut alongside other anniversary events. The original 1977 film will return to theaters on February 19, 2027, giving fans a chance to see the unaltered version on the big screen.

In addition, a new line of limited‑edition Retro action figures from Hasbro will launch exclusively at Target in spring 2027, echoing the classic Kenner toys that defined the 1970s. Tickets are also on sale for a museum exhibition that will showcase props, costumes, and behind‑the‑scenes material throughout the year.

The celebration culminates with the release of a new Star Wars movie, *Star Wars: Starfighter*, slated for May 28, 2027, and a series of fan events, park experiences, and publishing projects announced for the same year.

## Why it matters
The coordinated push reinforces Star Wars as a multi‑platform franchise, driving box‑office revenue, streaming subscriptions, and merchandise sales simultaneously. By pairing nostalgia (the theatrical re‑release and retro toys) with fresh content (the documentary and new film), Disney aims to capture both longtime fans and a younger audience.

The initiative also supports Disney’s broader strategy to leverage legacy IP across its parks, cruise lines, and streaming service, ensuring the brand remains a year‑round revenue engine rather than a seasonal spike.

## How to think about it
Treat the anniversary as a layered marketing funnel. Start with the high‑visibility theatrical re‑release to capture broad attention, then funnel interested viewers into the documentary and museum exhibition for deeper engagement. Finally, leverage limited‑edition merchandise and the new film to convert enthusiasm into spend. For fans, prioritize the re‑release if you want the classic experience, then plan for the documentary and exhibition based on personal interest and geographic availability.

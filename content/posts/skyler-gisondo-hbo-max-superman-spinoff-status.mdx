The People V. Gorilla Grodd, the upcoming HBO Max dramedy starring Skyler Gisondo as Jimmy Olsen, is currently in post‑production. The series' future has become a hot topic after James Gunn and Peter Safran announced they will step down as DC Studios co‑CEOs on Dec. 1, handing the reins to Jeremy Latcham. Gisondo addressed the uncertainty in a recent interview, expressing gratitude for the departing executives while admitting the situation is “above my pay grade.” Fans and industry watchers are watching closely because the series could be one of the few DCU projects still tied to Gunn and Safran’s original vision.

## What happened
James Gunn and Peter Safran will leave their roles as co‑CEOs of DC Studios on December 1, with Jeremy Latcham set to assume the top position. Their departure follows a turbulent year for the DCU, and both executives will continue as executive producers on several in‑development projects, including the Superman spinoff.
In an interview with ScreenRant, Skyler Gisondo, who is slated to reprise Jimmy Olsen, said the leadership change leaves him “above my pay grade,” but he praised Gunn and Safran, noting his gratitude for their work and hope the show will still move forward.
Gunn later posted on social media that, despite stepping down, he and Safran remain involved with DC projects, citing the upcoming “Man of Tomorrow” film and an unnamed DC script he is writing, suggesting the characters they introduced will continue to live on.
## Why it matters
The series is one of the few DCU‑era shows still in the pipeline, and its continuation could signal how much of Gunn and Safran’s creative slate survives under new leadership. A delay or cancellation would echo the Batgirl fallout, potentially eroding confidence in DC’s commitment to mid‑tier television projects. Conversely, green‑lighting the show would demonstrate continuity and reassure fans that the DCU’s tonal diversity remains intact.

Harry Potter HBO series is edging closer to its debut, and the latest casting reveal adds several iconic Hogwarts fixtures. Leo Bill has been confirmed as the ghost Nearly Headless Nick, while David Threlfall will provide the voice for the ever‑talkative Sorting Hat. The lineup also includes Andrew Brooke as the Bloody Baron, Daniel Fearn as the Fat Friar, and Laura Smyth as the portrait‑guarded Fat Lady. These announcements arrive just weeks before the season one premiere of “Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone.” Fans are buzzing about how these choices will shape the show’s tone.

<Callout type="takeaway">The new cast brings beloved Hogwarts spirits to life, promising a fresh yet faithful take on the wizarding world.</Callout>

## What happened
The official HBO and Harry Potter Instagram accounts posted a series of stills confirming the actors behind key Hogwarts fixtures. Leo Bill will portray Gryffindor’s ghost Nearly Headless Nick, a character known for his humorous after‑life mishap. David Threlfall, recognized for his work on “Broadchurch,” will voice the Sorting Hat, the sentient hat that assigns students to houses.

Andrew Brooke joins as the Slytherin ghost Bloody Baron, Daniel Fearn as Hufflepuff’s Fat Friar, and Laura Smyth steps into the role of the Fat Lady, the portrait that guards the Gryffindor common room entrance. The casting news follows earlier announcements of Charlie Weasley and the younger James and Lily Potter, and arrives alongside a new promotional poster that teases a refreshed visual of Hogwarts.

## Why it matters
Bringing these iconic spectral characters to screen signals HBO’s intent to deepen the magical world beyond the original film focus on student protagonists. Ghosts and the Sorting Hat provide opportunities for world‑building, humor, and exposition that were limited by the movies’ runtime. Their presence also reassures longtime fans that the series will honor the books’ beloved details while offering fresh performances.

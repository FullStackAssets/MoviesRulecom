Netflix announced that the 1993 Sylvester Stallone action thriller Cliffhanger is now streaming on its platform. The film, which follows a mountain‑rescue expert battling thieves over $100 million in scattered loot, debuted on Netflix on November 1, 2026. Its arrival gives a new generation easy access to a cult classic that once grossed $255 million worldwide on a $70 million budget. With Stallone’s silent‑hero performance and Renny Harlin’s high‑altitude set pieces, the movie’s return also sparks talk of a potential remake.

## What happened
Netflix added Cliffhanger to its catalog on November 1, 2026, after securing streaming rights.

The film’s plot involves a plane heist, an explosion, and three suitcases with $100 million scattered across the mountains, forcing Stallone’s character and his former partner to use climbing skills to retrieve the money.

Directed by Renny Harlin, the original 1993 release earned $255 million worldwide against a $70 million budget and has become a ’90s cult classic.

## Why it matters
Bringing Cliffhanger to a streaming platform expands its audience beyond the original theater‑goers, introducing the high‑octane action and iconic Stallone performance to younger viewers. The move also demonstrates Netflix’s strategy of leveraging legacy titles to boost its catalog depth, while the buzz around a possible remake highlights how classic franchises can be revitalized for modern markets. For fans, the streaming debut offers a convenient way to revisit a beloved film, and for the industry, it signals continued interest in repurposing ’90s action properties.

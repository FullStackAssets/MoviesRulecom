Holiday season is here, and streaming platforms have rolled out a packed lineup of movies, series, and specials for 2026. Rotten Tomatoes' latest guide lists new releases on Hallmark+, Apple TV+, Disney+, Netflix, and dozens of other services. From classic favorites like "Home Alone" on Disney+ to fresh originals such as "Home for Christmas" on Apple TV+, the schedule covers every taste. With families planning binge‑watch sessions and advertisers vying for holiday attention, knowing where to find the best content matters. This guide helps viewers navigate the overload and pick the right titles for their celebrations.

<Callout type="takeaway">The 2026 holiday guide shows every major streaming service delivering a mix of classics and new originals, so you can plan your festive watchlist in advance.</Callout>

## What happened
Rotten Tomatoes compiled a comprehensive schedule that spans cable networks and streaming platforms, noting start dates as early as October for Hallmark+ and Great American Family. The guide groups titles by service, highlighting hubs such as Disney+ "Happy Holidays" and Netflix "Holiday Magic" that aggregate seasonal content.

Key entries include AMC+ featuring "Elf" and "White Christmas," Apple TV+ offering "Home for Christmas" and Mariah Carey’s special, Disney+ streaming "Home Alone" and "The Nightmare Before Christmas," and Netflix showcasing "The Christmas Chronicles" and "That Christmas." Other services like Hulu, Prime Video, and Peacock add their own holiday episodes from popular series.

## Why it matters
The holiday rush intensifies competition for subscriber attention, and a curated guide reduces friction for viewers overwhelmed by fragmented libraries. Networks use seasonal programming to boost ad revenue, while streaming services leverage exclusive originals to retain and attract subscribers during the most-watched weeks of the year. Understanding the lineup helps families plan shared viewing and allows marketers to target holiday‑mood audiences more effectively.

## How to think about it
Start by inventorying the streaming services you already subscribe to, then cross‑reference the guide to identify must‑watch titles that are exclusive to a platform you don’t have. Prioritize classics you’ve missed and new originals that align with your family’s interests. Use each service’s holiday hub or playlist feature to create a watchlist, and set calendar reminders for premiere dates to avoid missing limited‑time releases.

<Callout type="warning">Remember that many of the highlighted titles require an active subscription or rental fee; they are not available for free on all platforms.</Callout>

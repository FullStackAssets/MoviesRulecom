The highly anticipated sixth season of Hulu’s murder‑mystery comedy Only Murders in the Building is finally on the calendar. Deadline confirms the series will debut on December 8, 2026, and that the trio of New York sleuths will trade the Arconia for the streets of London. The move introduces a wave of British and Irish talent, from Olivia Colman to David Tennant, expanding the show’s comedic palette. Fans are also getting a fresh trailer that teases the new setting and guest stars. This shift could reshape the series’ tone and broaden its international appeal.
<Callout type="takeaway">Season 6 launches Dec 8, heads to London, and adds a star‑studded British‑Irish cast.</Callout>
## What happened
Deadline reported that Hulu officially renewed Only Murders in the Building for a sixth season shortly after the Season 5 finale and set the premiere for December 8, 2026. A new trailer has been released, confirming that the story will move overseas, with London serving as the primary backdrop for the mystery.
The season brings a sizable influx of guest talent. Confirmed additions include Olivia Colman, Peter Capaldi, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan, Jodie Whittaker, Jim Broadbent, Richard Ayoade, and several others such as Jennifer Saunders and Sharon Horgan, many of whom were announced via social‑media posts and industry reports.
## Why it matters
Shifting the setting to London opens narrative possibilities and visual diversity, allowing the show to explore trans‑Atlantic cultural jokes while keeping its core investigative formula. The high‑profile British‑Irish cast not only broadens the series’ appeal to international audiences but also signals Hulu’s intent to compete with other global streaming hits. However, the change also tests the chemistry of the original trio in a new environment, risking a departure from the New York‑centric charm that defined earlier seasons.

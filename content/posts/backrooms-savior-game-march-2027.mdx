The cult horror film Backrooms is getting a new life beyond the silver screen. A24 announced that a first‑person shooter titled Backrooms: Savior will launch on PC in March 2027. The game lets players wander the infamous endless corridors armed for the first time. By moving the franchise into interactive media, the studio hopes to capture both film fans and gamers. The announcement arrives just weeks after the original movie’s $400 million box‑office run.

<Callout type="takeaway">Backrooms: Savior turns the franchise’s terrifying setting into an armed, player‑driven experience in 2027.</Callout>

## What happened
A24 confirmed that Backrooms: Savior, a new shooter based on the 2026 film Backrooms, will be released on PC in March 2027. The game will let players explore the maze‑like environments that made the movie a horror hit, this time with weapons and combat mechanics.

The original Backrooms film, directed by 20‑year‑old Kane Parsons and starring Oscar‑nominated Chiwetel Ejiofor, cost $10 million to produce and grossed $400 million worldwide. Its success has spurred interest in expanding the brand, and the upcoming game is the first major interactive project tied to the franchise.

## Why it matters
The move from a single‑screen horror experience to an interactive shooter signals A24’s intent to turn Backrooms into a multi‑platform franchise. It gives fans a way to engage directly with the unsettling setting, potentially extending the series’ cultural relevance. At the same time, the game’s performance will test whether the Backrooms mythos can sustain a broader audience beyond cinema.

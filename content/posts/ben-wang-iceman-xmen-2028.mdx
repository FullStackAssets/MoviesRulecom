Ben Wang, known for his roles in 'Karate Kid: Legends' and Disney+ hits, is now being linked to Marvel’s next X-Men film. Sources tell Deadline that the actor is in talks to take on the iconic mutant Iceman, also known as Bobby Drake. The X-Men project, directed by Jake Schreier and produced by Kevin Feige, is scheduled for a May 5, 2028 theatrical release. If confirmed, Wang would join a star‑studded cast that includes Christopher Abbott, Adam Driver and Kit Connor, expanding his profile in blockbuster cinema.

## What happened
Deadline reports that Ben Wang is circling talks to portray Iceman in Marvel Studios’ upcoming X-Men movie, though Marvel has not issued an official comment. The film, set for release on May 5, 2028, will be directed by Jake Schreier from a script by Lee Sung Jin and Joanna Calo, and will feature an ensemble cast announced at D23, including Christopher Abbott as Professor X and Adam Driver as Mr. Sinister. Wang’s recent credits span the Disney+ series American Born Chinese, the Lionsgate adaptation The Long Walk, and the upcoming Apple TV+ season of For All Mankind, positioning him as a rising talent ready for a superhero franchise.

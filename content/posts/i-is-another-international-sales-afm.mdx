The new historical drama *I Is Another* starring Claes Bang and Valerie Pachner has just locked in a slate of international sales deals ahead of the American Film Market. Beta Cinema announced agreements spanning Europe, Asia and Oceania, turning the film into a truly global title. This momentum follows its world premiere on Locarno’s Piazza Grande this summer and sets the stage for a high‑profile AFM screening. For indie‑drama fans, the breadth of the deals signals a rare chance to see a niche European story reach diverse audiences.

<Callout type="takeaway">‘I Is Another’ now has a global distribution pipeline before its AFM debut.</Callout>

## What happened
Beta Cinema closed sales with ARP Selection for France, Youplanet Pictures for Spain, Angel Films for Scandinavia, Big Tree Entertainment for India, and Palace Films for Australia and New Zealand. Additional territories include Vertigo Media (Hungary), Beta Film (Bulgaria), Red Cape (Israel) and Port‑au‑Prince Pictures, which will release the film in Germany in early 2027. The deals were unveiled on the eve of AFM, and the film will screen at the market on Nov. 11 at AMC Century City 3.

The film, written and directed by Felix Randau, follows Felix Kersten—played by Bang—a massage therapist to SS leader Heinrich Himmler who claims to have helped free thousands of concentration‑camp prisoners. On a night train to Oslo, he meets journalist Hedda (Pachner), sparking a duel over memory, truth and the legacy Kersten wishes to leave.

## Why it matters
Securing territory sales before AFM demonstrates strong buyer confidence in a period piece that blends historical intrigue with personal drama. The wide geographic spread gives the film multiple theatrical windows, increasing its visibility and potential for later streaming deals. For audiences, this means the story may appear on regional platforms sooner than a typical art‑house release that relies on a single territory distributor.

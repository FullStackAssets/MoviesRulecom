beIN Media Group has secured a multi‑year output agreement with NBCUniversal Global, granting exclusive rights to a slate of recent blockbusters and acclaimed dramas for the Middle East and North Africa. The deal includes the theatrical hits Michael and Jurassic World Rebirth, the award‑winning film Hamnet, and a range of popular franchises such as Fast & Furious and How to Train Your Dragon. By adding these titles to its portfolio, beIN expands its premium content offering and strengthens its position as a leading entertainment provider in the region.

<Callout type="takeaway">beIN’s partnership with NBCUniversal brings high‑profile Hollywood titles to MENA viewers, boosting the channel’s competitive edge.</Callout>

## What happened
The agreement was announced at the Doha Industry Days 2026 and gives beIN exclusive rights to NBCUniversal’s Pay‑1 films, first‑run series, Peacock originals, and a vast library of classic shows. The slate covers recent releases such as Michael, Jurassic World Rebirth, and Hamnet, as well as franchise hits like Fast & Furious, How to Train Your Dragon, Minions, Shrek, and Downton Abbey. Additional titles include The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, Wicked: For Good, the legal drama War, and complete libraries of The Office and Suits.

## Why it matters
By securing these titles, beIN can offer a broader range of premium content, appealing to both casual viewers and cinephiles. The partnership positions beIN to compete more directly with global streaming services that already host NBCUniversal content, potentially driving subscriber growth in the MENA market. It also reflects a broader trend of regional broadcasters seeking high‑profile Hollywood content to differentiate themselves.

## How to think about it
When evaluating such deals, broadcasters should weigh the value of exclusive high‑profile titles against the cost of licensing and the risk of cannibalizing existing content libraries. A balanced approach that blends global hits with locally relevant programming can help sustain subscriber growth while managing budgets.

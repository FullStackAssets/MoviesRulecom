## What happened
Deadline reported that Nicolas Cage and Chris Pine have been cast as the leads of American Epic, an A24‑produced series centered on the high‑risk world of casino operators. The series is written by B.J. Novak, known for his work on The Office and other comedy‑drama hybrids. A24 is shopping the project to a range of broadcasters and streaming platforms, but no distribution deal has been confirmed yet.

Production details remain under wraps, but the involvement of A24 suggests a stylized, auteur‑driven approach, while the Vegas setting hints at a blend of crime, glamour, and character study.

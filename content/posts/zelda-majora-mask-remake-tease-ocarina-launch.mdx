Fans of The Legend of Zelda got a double dose of excitement this week: Nintendo released the long‑awaited Ocarina of Time remake and, within the Nintendo Today app, slipped a seven‑second animated teaser for Majora’s Mask. The Ocarina remake has already topped Metacritic with a 96 rating, confirming strong critical reception. The brief animation shows Young Link and other characters in full motion, visible only to users who set the app’s theme to Zelda. This hint suggests Nintendo may be lining up a Majora’s Mask remake as its next major release. The possibility reshapes expectations for the franchise’s future and Nintendo’s remake strategy.

<Callout type="takeaway">Majora’s Mask is likely Nintendo’s next Zelda remake, building on Ocarina’s success.</Callout>

## What happened
The Ocarina of Time remake launched at the start of November, updating visuals and adding quality‑of‑life features while preserving core gameplay. Critics have responded enthusiastically, giving the title a Metacritic score of 96, the highest rating of the past year. The strong reception demonstrates the market appetite for refreshed classic Zelda entries.

Within the Nintendo Today app, a newly animated seven‑second clip of Majora’s Mask characters appeared, featuring Young Link at the center. The animation is only visible to users who select The Legend of Zelda as the app’s overall theme, a free setting change. Additionally, the IMDb page for adult Link voice actor Nobuyuki Hiyama was updated to list an unannounced game in post‑production, further fueling speculation.

## Why it matters
The teaser signals that Nintendo may be planning to follow Ocarina’s commercial triumph with a Majora’s Mask remake, a title known for its darker tone and innovative mechanics. A new remake could attract both longtime fans and newcomers, extending the franchise’s revenue stream without the risk of a brand‑new IP. It also sets a precedent for how Nintendo leverages its existing catalog to sustain interest between original releases.

## How to think about it
Treat the teaser as an early indicator rather than a confirmation. Keep an eye on official Nintendo communications, monitor the Nintendo Today app for further hints, and consider how a Majora’s Mask remake would fit into your gaming schedule and budget.

<Callout type="warning">Do not assume the remake is confirmed until Nintendo makes an official announcement.</Callout>

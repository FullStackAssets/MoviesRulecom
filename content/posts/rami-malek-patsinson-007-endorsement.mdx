Rami Malek, the former Bond villain who played Lyutsifer Safin, publicly praised Robert Pattinson’s appointment as the next 007. The actor said he “could not be more proud” of Pattinson’s talent and that the world will see him as an extraordinary spy. Malek’s endorsement comes after Pattinson’s announcement, which ended months of speculation over who would inherit the iconic role. Fans and industry insiders alike are watching closely as Amazon MGM prepares to launch the new franchise chapter.

<Callout type="takeaway">Rami Malek’s approval signals confidence in Pattinson’s ability to carry the Bond legacy.</Callout>

## What happened

Malek first expressed his support in an interview with People, noting their friendship from their 2012 Twilight film. He highlighted Pattinson’s talent and said he and Pattinson have been texting since the casting news.

Pattinson’s casting followed a long list of rumored actors, including Callum Turner and Harry Styles. Denis Villeneuve will direct the upcoming film, with Amazon MGM Studios handling production.

## Why it matters

Malek’s praise carries weight because he was part of the 2021 Bond film, and his perspective may reassure fans about the franchise’s direction. Pattinson’s role also marks a shift toward a more introspective, character-driven Bond, potentially expanding the series’ appeal. The endorsement could influence audience expectations and box‑office performance.

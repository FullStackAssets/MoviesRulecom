## What happened

The list includes 'Anatomy of a Fall' (2023), directed by Justine Triet, which uses a courtroom drama format to dissect a fractured marriage. The film centers on Sandra Voyter, played by Sandra Hüller, who defends herself after her husband is found dead outside their chalet in the French Alps. The narrative relies on a visually impaired son as the primary witness, creating a puzzle where every detail of the couple's relationship is scrutinized.

The research notes that while many thrillers use shocking reveals to reframe the narrative, the most successful ones keep the audience desperate to know what happens next. The procedural elements of the trial serve to highlight the emotional ambiguity of the characters, ensuring the tension remains rooted in human complexity.

## How to think about it
When evaluating a thriller, look for how the film manages information. Does it withhold key details to create mystery, or does it reveal them slowly to build dread? A strong suspenseful film often makes the audience complicit in the uncertainty, forcing them to question the reliability of the characters or the narrative itself. Instead of waiting for a single climactic twist, pay attention to the pacing and the emotional stakes of the characters. If the film keeps you questioning the motives and relationships of the protagonists throughout the runtime, it is likely succeeding in its primary goal of sustaining suspense.

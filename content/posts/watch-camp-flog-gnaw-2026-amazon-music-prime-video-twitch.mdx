## What happened
On November 11, 2026, Amazon announced that the Camp Flog Gnaw festival will be livestreamed at no charge across three of its services. The fourth consecutive year Amazon Music holds exclusive livestream rights, while Prime Video and Twitch also carry the feed.
The festival showcases more than 40 artists spanning hip‑hop, R&B, rock, and other genres, continuing Tyler, The Creator’s tradition of eclectic lineups. Viewers can switch between the platforms to catch different camera angles or exclusive backstage content.
## Why it matters
Free, multi‑platform streaming expands the festival’s reach beyond the physical venue, reinforcing Amazon’s push to dominate live entertainment online. It also gives emerging artists exposure to a global audience, while fans benefit from flexible viewing options without ticket costs.

## How to think about it
First, create or sign into your Amazon account, then open the Amazon Music app, Prime Video app, or Twitch website at the scheduled start. Verify your internet connection can handle HD streams, and keep the app updated to avoid glitches. If you prefer a single view, pick the platform you already use most, but keep the others handy for backup if one experiences lag.
<Callout type="warning">Make sure you’re in a supported region; attempting to watch from unsupported locations will result in a black screen.</Callout>

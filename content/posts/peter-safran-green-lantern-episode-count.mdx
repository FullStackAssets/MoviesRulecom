The DC Universe is expanding its animated lineup with a fresh take on the iconic lantern. Co‑CEO Peter Safran confirmed to Collider that the upcoming series My Adventures with Green Lantern will consist of ten half‑hour episodes. The show is slated to share the light‑hearted tone of My Adventures with Superman while exploring new thematic ground. This clarification arrives as fans scramble for details on the series’ format and its place in DC Studios’ broader streaming strategy.

<Callout type="takeaway">My Adventures with Green Lantern is set to debut as a ten‑episode, half‑hour animated series.</Callout>

## What happened
Peter Safran told Collider that My Adventures with Green Lantern will run for ten half‑hour episodes, a concise format that differs from the hour‑long structures of many previous DC animated shows. He also noted that the series will be tonally similar to My Adventures with Superman, aiming for a light, comedic feel while tackling distinct thematic material.

The studio is currently reviewing animatics and animation, indicating that production is well underway despite the lack of an official release date. This update provides the first concrete episode count for the project, which had previously been shrouded in speculation.

## Why it matters
A ten‑episode, half‑hour model aligns with the binge‑friendly habits of streaming audiences, allowing viewers to consume the series quickly without a massive time commitment. It also signals DC Studios’ strategy to diversify its animated slate, offering both long‑form epics and shorter, more experimental titles. For fans of the lantern mythos, the clarified format promises a focused narrative that can explore character growth without filler.

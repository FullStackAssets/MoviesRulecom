## What happened
The film, directed by Kris Rey and co‑written by Hearon, premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival, where it received positive reviews. It stars Deschanel as half‑sister Abbi, Hearon as protagonist Gavin, and a supporting cast that includes Jacki Weaver and Jaboukie Young‑White. The production was backed by Metronome Film Company and several independent partners.

Magnolia Pictures announced the acquisition on November 19, 2026, stating the film is ‘funny, smart, and joyful.’ The distributor plans a theatrical release for next year, with plans for a potential streaming window thereafter. The film’s 100% fresh Rotten Tomatoes score underscores its critical success.

## Why it matters
The deal highlights Magnolia’s continued support for indie narratives that tackle LGBTQ+ stories with humor. It also offers a rare theatrical platform for a film that blends comedy with poignant family drama. For fans of Deschanel and Hearon, the release provides a tangible way to experience their collaboration beyond festival circuits. Moreover, the acquisition could influence how other distributors approach similar niche films.

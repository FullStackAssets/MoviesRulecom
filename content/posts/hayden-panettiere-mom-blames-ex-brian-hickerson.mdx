Hayden Panettiere’s mother, Lesley Vogel, appeared on Inside Edition this week to lay blame for her daughter’s death on the star’s former boyfriend, Brian Hickerson. In the interview, Vogel said there is “no doubt” that Hickerson is responsible for the estrangement that preceded Panettiere’s accidental overdose. The comments come as the family remains divided over funeral arrangements and public statements. Fans and industry observers are watching to see how the narrative may shape the actress’s legacy.

## What happened
Lesley Vogel told Inside Edition that she believes Brian Hickerson is “absolutely” responsible for the breakdown in her relationship with Hayden, describing the estrangement as a direct result of his influence. Vogel explained that she skipped Panettiere’s Malibu memorial because she feared being painted as the “horrible mother” and wanted to avoid further conflict.

The actress’s death was ruled an accidental overdose, and her father attended the service alongside co‑stars Connie Britton and Charles Esten. Vogel provided a copy of the invitation to Inside Edition, asserting she was not excluded, while also noting that she has not spoken to her daughter since the fall of 2025.

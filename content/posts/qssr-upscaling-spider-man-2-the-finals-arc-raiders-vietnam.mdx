Spider-Man fans and PS5 owners have a new reason to revisit their favorite titles.  Sony announced that Quick Spectral Super Resolution (QSSR) is being rolled out to several flagship games, including Spider-Man 2, The Finals, ARC Raiders, and the upcoming Hell Let Loose: Vietnam.  QSSR builds on the same AI‑guided pipeline as PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) but works on the base PS5, promising sharper details and more stable images.  The updates arrive as free patches, meaning players can enable the technology without buying new hardware.

<Callout type="takeaway">QSSR brings sharper, more stable visuals to several flagship PS5 titles, extending the console’s lifespan.</Callout>

## What happened
Sony introduced QSSR last month as an AI‑driven upscaler for the standard PS5, leveraging the same architecture that powers PSSR on the PS5 Pro.  The company confirmed that the first wave of games will receive the technology via upcoming patches, starting with Marvel’s Spider‑Man 2, which already supports PSSR.  Insomniac’s Mike Fitzgerald noted that the update adds “image stability and sharpness benefits in both Fidelity and Performance modes.”

Remedy Entertainment is also adding QSSR to Control Resonant, with senior graphics programmer Erik Jansson saying the update delivers a “much more stable and detailed image.”  Embark Studios confirmed the same for The Finals and ARC Raiders, highlighting increased sharpness and clearer environmental detail.  Hell Let Loose: Vietnam will join the list, bringing AI‑upscaled visuals to the historic shooter when its update lands.

## Why it matters
The rollout shows Sony’s commitment to extending the PS5’s relevance well beyond its launch window.  By delivering console‑wide AI upscaling, developers can push higher fidelity without sacrificing frame rates, giving players a more immersive experience on existing hardware.  It also levels the playing field between PS5 owners and PS5 Pro users, as both can now access similar image‑quality gains.

## How to think about it
First, ensure your console is running the latest system software.  Then check each game's update notes for a QSSR option in the graphics settings menu.  Enable the feature and compare the Fidelity and Performance presets to see which balance of visual quality and frame rate suits your play style.  If you notice any dips in performance, you can toggle QSSR off or switch to a lower resolution mode.

<Callout type="warning">Not every PS5 title supports QSSR yet, so avoid assuming universal improvement across your library.</Callout>

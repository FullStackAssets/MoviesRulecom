When the AI titan Anthropic went public, the world watched its valuation skyrocket.
For Hollywood actor‑turned‑VC Ashton Kutcher, the IPO was more than a headline; it could catapult him into the billionaire club.
Sound Ventures, the firm he co‑founded, saw its 0.5% stake soar to roughly $7 billion.
The move underscores how savvy tech investing can eclipse traditional acting income.
Fans will now see a different side of Kutcher’s portfolio.

<Callout type="takeaway">Kutcher’s Anthropic stake could push his net worth past $7 billion, making him a bona‑fide billionaire.</Callout>

## What happened

Sound Ventures, co‑founded by Kutcher and Guy Oseary, invested $435 million in Anthropic in 2023 and now holds 0.5% of the company.
The IPO priced Anthropic at a valuation of over $1.5 trillion, turning the stake into an estimated $7 billion.
The company’s debut also marked the first major AI firm to go public, drawing attention from investors and regulators alike.

Sound Ventures left earlier this year to launch Decimal Capital, a new tech‑focused VC, while remaining a senior adviser.
Prior to the IPO, his net worth was estimated above $500 million, largely from stakes in Spotify, Uber, Airbnb, and the now‑acquired Hugging Face.
The Anthropic windfall is the latest in a series of high‑yield investments that have positioned him as a serious player in the tech ecosystem.

## Why it matters

The surge in Kutcher’s wealth illustrates the power of early‑stage AI investing and the growing intersection between entertainment and technology.
It also raises questions about the influence of celebrity investors on emerging AI companies and the potential for conflicts of interest.
For the broader market, the IPO signals that AI firms can command valuations comparable to traditional tech giants, potentially reshaping funding landscapes.

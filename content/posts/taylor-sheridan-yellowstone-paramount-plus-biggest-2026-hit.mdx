Taylor Sheridan’s Yellowstone has officially been crowned Paramount+’s biggest hit of 2026, maintaining a spot in the platform’s global top‑10 for more than 300 days. The five‑season series, which ran from 2018 to 2024, continues to dominate watchlists despite concluding last year. Its endurance signals a rare streaming milestone for a western‑themed drama and reinforces Sheridan’s influence across film and television. Industry analysts see the achievement as a barometer for long‑form genre success on subscription services.

<Callout type="takeaway">Yellowstone’s streaming longevity proves that genre storytelling can dominate global platforms.</Callout>

## What happened
Yellowstone stayed in Paramount+’s top‑10 for over 300 days in 2026, a feat achieved by only a handful of titles worldwide. The show’s five‑season run, created by Taylor Sheridan, originally aired from 2018 through 2024 and has since become a staple of the platform’s catalog. Its continued popularity is reflected in global viewership metrics that place it ahead of newer competitors on the service.

## Why it matters
The sustained performance highlights the commercial viability of western narratives in a streaming‑driven market, encouraging platforms to invest in similar genre projects. It also cements Sheridan’s reputation as a creator whose work transcends traditional TV lifespans, influencing future development deals. For subscribers, the success reinforces the value of long‑form storytelling as a key driver of platform loyalty.

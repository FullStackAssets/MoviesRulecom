The beach‑life drama that defined 1990s TV is back. Fox announced that the new 'Baywatch' reboot will debut on Jan. 6, 2026 at 9 p.m., starring Stephen Amell as a grown‑up Hobie Buchannon. The series adds a fresh family twist with Jessica Belkin playing Charlie, Hobie's long‑lost daughter who wants to follow in his lifeguard footsteps. With a 12‑episode order for the 2026‑27 season, the show is positioned to bring sunshine to the winter lineup.
<Callout type="takeaway">The reboot aims to revive the iconic beach drama for a new winter audience.</Callout>
## What happened
Fox confirmed the premiere date and time, noting the series will roll out on Jan. 6, 2026 at 9 p.m. on the network. Stephen Amell leads as Hobie Buchannon, now a Baywatch captain, while Jessica Belkin joins as his daughter Charlie. The ensemble also includes Shay Mitchell, Brooks Nader, Hassie Harrison, and a cameo by Megan Thee Stallion.
The reboot was picked up in September 2025 with a 12‑episode order for the 2026‑27 broadcast season. Production began in Los Angeles in spring 2026 after an open casting call earlier in the year. Executive producers include Matt Nix, McG, Michael Berk, Greg Bonann, Doug Schwartz, Dante Di Loreto, and Mike Horowitz, with McG directing the pilot.
## Why it matters
Fox is using the beloved brand to bolster its winter lineup, offering bright, escapist content when audiences typically seek indoor programming. The combination of a recognizable franchise and a modern star like Amell aims to attract both nostalgic viewers and a younger demographic, positioning the network against streaming services that dominate new‑season premieres. Success could signal a broader trend of legacy TV revivals seeking prime‑time slots rather than streaming exclusives.

HBO Max adds a fresh lineup for the week of Nov 13, 2026, spotlighting a true‑crime docuseries, a culinary road‑trip series, and a hard‑hitting short about opioid addiction.
The four‑part series Clangold recounts the 2017 heist of the massive “Big Maple Leaf” gold coin from Berlin’s Bode Museum.
Detour with David Chang follows the celebrity chef as he explores diverse food scenes across America.
The Hotline offers a brief but powerful look at a peer‑support hotline for addicts.
Together, these titles expand the platform’s mix of investigative, lifestyle, and socially relevant content.
<Callout type="takeaway">HBO Max’s new week blends true crime, culinary travel, and urgent social documentary.</Callout>
## What happened
Clangold is a four‑part documentary that dives into the robbery of the 220‑pound gold coin known as the “Big Maple Leaf,” detailing the investigation that led police to the Mhallami‑Arab Remmo family. The series features interviews with key figures such as Ali Reza Ahmadi, Omar Antabli, and Cenk Bayram Babaoglu, offering a layered view of the crime and its legal fallout.
Detour with David Chang, a six‑episode CNN series, sees the restaurateur traveling to culinary hotspots like South Florida and Pennsylvania. Each episode pairs Chang with local personalities—Eric André, Gillian Jacobs, among others—to sample regional dishes and discuss food culture.
The Hotline is a short documentary that goes behind the scenes of an opioid‑addiction support line called Never Use Alone, highlighting how former addicts provide real‑time assistance and emergency response to callers in crisis.
## Why it matters
These releases illustrate HBO Max’s strategy to diversify its catalog beyond scripted drama, appealing to viewers seeking factual, experiential, and socially conscious content. True‑crime fans get a deep dive into an international heist, food lovers receive a travel‑infused culinary adventure, and audiences concerned with public health see a concise look at harm‑reduction efforts. By mixing genres, the platform can attract distinct audience segments and keep subscription churn low.

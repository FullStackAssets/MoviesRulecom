Hot Toys has just unveiled its new 1/6‑scale Beast collectible, modeled after Kelsey Grammer’s version of the X‑Men mutant in the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday. The figure stands about 32.5 cm tall, sports a shirtless look with a yellow‑belted X‑Men logo, and includes 30 points of articulation. Fans of the crossover project now have a concrete glimpse of the character’s design before the film hits theaters on December 18, 2026. The reveal also hints at the timing of the figure’s release, slated for the latter half of 2027.
<Callout type="takeaway">The new Beast figure offers the most detailed look yet at Grammer’s Avengers: Doomsday costume.</Callout>
## What happened
Hot Toys announced a 1/6th‑scale Beast figure that captures the mutant’s appearance in Avengers: Doomsday. The collectible features a refined head sculpt with rolling eyeballs, dark blue pants with yellow accents, and a yellow belt bearing the X‑Men logo. It also includes interchangeable hands—fists, claws, and grabbing gestures—and a stand emblazoned with the Avengers: Doomsday logo.
The company said the figure will be released sometime between the last two quarters of 2027, aligning with the scheduled launch of Avengers: Secret Wars on December 17, 2027. This places the toy’s market debut roughly a year after the film’s December 18, 2026 release.
## Why it matters
The detailed figure gives collectors and fans a tangible preview of Beast’s updated look, reinforcing the crossover’s marketing momentum even before official trailers feature the character. Its timing also tests Hot Toys’ ability to synchronize product drops with Marvel’s release calendar, a strategy that can drive both merchandise sales and audience anticipation for the film.

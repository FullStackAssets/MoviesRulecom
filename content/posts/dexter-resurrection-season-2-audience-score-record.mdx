Fans of the gritty New York vigilante were treated to a surprise when Dexter: Resurrection season 2 dropped on October 30. Within days, audience members on Rotten Tomatoes poured in more than 50 reviews, pushing the score to an astonishing 98%. The surge not only eclipses previous entries in the Dexter franchise but also signals a rare moment of near‑universal fan approval for a revived series. As streaming platforms scramble for hit content, this record could reshape expectations for legacy shows.

<Callout type="takeaway">Dexter: Resurrection season 2 has set a franchise‑wide audience approval record with a 98% Rotten Tomatoes rating.</Callout>

## What happened
The second season of Dexter: Resurrection premiered on October 30 on the streaming service, continuing the story of Dexter Morgan’s return to New York. Within the first week, Rotten Tomatoes logged over 50 audience reviews, resulting in a 98% audience score—the highest any Dexter installment has achieved on the site. Earlier seasons hovered in the 70‑80% range, making this jump especially notable.

## Why it matters
A near‑perfect audience rating can boost a show's visibility, attract new subscribers, and give the platform leverage for future renewals. For a franchise that struggled to recapture its original spark, the score suggests a successful reinvention that resonates with both longtime fans and newcomers. It also puts pressure on critics and creators to maintain the momentum, as expectations for subsequent seasons will now be set at an unusually high bar.

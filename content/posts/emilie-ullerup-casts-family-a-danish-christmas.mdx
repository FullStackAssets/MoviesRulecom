Hallmark Channel’s latest holiday offering, “A Danish Christmas,” is turning heads for a very personal reason. Actress Emilie Ullerup, who also serves as executive producer, invited her mother, cousins, sisters, nephew and husband to appear on screen. Co‑star Corey Sevier brought his wife and two children along for the Copenhagen shoot. The family‑filled cast adds an authentic Danish flavor to the romantic‑comedy formula. Fans are eager to spot the real‑life relatives in the festive storyline.

<Callout type="takeaway">Ullerup’s decision to cast her own family turns a standard Hallmark romance into a genuine family celebration.</Callout>

## What happened
Filming took place in Copenhagen this past December, with Hallmark’s production team coordinating schedules to accommodate the extended family crew. The movie, executive‑produced by Ullerup, premiered on Hallmark Channel on November 1, 2026, and features cameo appearances by her mother, a cousin who plays her HR boss, her sisters, nephew and husband Kyle Cassie, who previously appeared opposite her on Chesapeake Shores.

Corey Sevier also shared the experience with his wife, screenwriter Kate Pragnell, and their two‑year‑old and seven‑year‑old children, who enjoyed local pastries and a visit to Tivoli Gardens. Their on‑set presence was highlighted in promotional interviews, emphasizing the personal nature of the production.

## Why it matters
The casting choice blurs the line between on‑screen storytelling and real‑life family dynamics, giving the film an organic warmth that resonates with Hallmark’s audience. It also serves as a marketing hook, generating buzz on social media as viewers hunt for familiar faces. For Hallmark, showcasing authentic Danish culture through a home‑grown cast supports its global expansion strategy while reinforcing the network’s reputation for feel‑good, family‑centric content.

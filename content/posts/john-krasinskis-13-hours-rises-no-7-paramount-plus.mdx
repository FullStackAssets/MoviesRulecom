John Krasinski’s 144‑minute action thriller 13 Hours: The Secret Soldiers of Benghazi has surged onto Paramount+’s US Top 10, landing at number 7 on November 2. The film, directed by Michael Bay and starring Krasinski as a member of the Annex Security Team, suddenly outperformed newer releases such as One Mile: Chapter One and Dracula. Its unexpected streaming lift comes despite a modest 51 % Rotten Tomatoes score and a historically low box‑office performance. The jump signals how streaming platforms can revive titles that struggled in theaters. Fans and industry watchers are taking note of the shift.

<Callout type="takeaway">Streaming can turn a box‑office flop into a top‑ranked title.</Callout>

## What happened
13 Hours: The Secret Soldiers of Benghazi, a 144‑minute action thriller directed by Michael Bay, stars John Krasinski as one of six Annex Security Team members defending the U.S. diplomatic compound in Benghazi, Libya. After arriving on Paramount+ earlier this week, the title shot up to No. 7 on the service’s United States Top 10 list for November 2, sitting behind One Mile: Chapter One, Dracula, and One Mile: Chapter Two, while edging out titles like We’re the Millers and Norbit.

The film’s resurgence is notable because it originally earned a 51 % Rotten Tomatoes rating and was one of Michael Bay’s lower‑grossing theatrical releases. Its new streaming momentum demonstrates that audience interest can be reignited long after a movie’s initial box‑office run, especially when a recognizable star like Krasinski is attached.

## Why it matters
The climb illustrates the power of streaming platforms to reshape a film’s lifecycle, turning a modestly received theatrical effort into a headline‑making title. For Paramount+, promoting a recognizable action thriller bolsters its library credibility and can attract viewers seeking high‑stakes content. For Krasinski, the renewed visibility reinforces his transition from sitcom star to action‑genre lead, potentially influencing casting decisions and future project financing.

Ron Livingston, best known for Office Space, has wrapped principal photography on Steve Collins’s new indie comedy “Fonk” in Ohio. The film also stars rising star Jake Ryan, veteran character actress Beth Grant, and Christina Kirk. “Fonk” follows a disgraced English professor who hijacks a student’s thesis film to launch his own unfilmable novel. The completion of the shoot marks a key milestone for the Eyeheart Cinema and Ten Acre Films collaboration, positioning the project for festival circuits and potential streaming deals.

## What happened
The Ohio shoot for “Fonk” wrapped this week, bringing together an ensemble that includes Ron Livingston as Professor Fonk, Jake Ryan as his protégé, Beth Grant as his mother, and Christina Kirk as his ex‑wife. The production, a joint effort by Eyeheart Cinema and Ten Acre Films, was overseen by producers Jonathan Duffy and Kelly Williams, known for “Sorry to Bother You” and “What Happens Later”. Executive producers include Livingston himself and Derk Cullinan.

The story centers on a beleaguered English professor who commandeers an impressionable student’s thesis film, turning it into a chaotic partnership aimed at salvaging his dignity and filming his “unfilmable” debut novel. Director‑writer Steve Collins, whose previous work includes “Gretchen” and “I’ve Got Issues”, helms the project, bringing his off‑beat comedic sensibility to the script.

## Why it matters
“Fonk” adds to a growing slate of indie comedies that blend meta‑narrative humor with character‑driven storytelling, a formula that has resonated on the festival circuit and with streaming platforms. The involvement of recognizable talent like Livingston and Ryan increases the film’s marketability, potentially easing its path to a wider audience. Moreover, the project showcases the continued vitality of regional production hubs such as Ohio, highlighting how mid‑budget films can thrive outside traditional Hollywood locales.

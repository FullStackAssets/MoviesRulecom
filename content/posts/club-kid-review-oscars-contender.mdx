## What happened
Club Kid premiered at Cannes, sparking a bidding war that A24 won. The film was released in a limited theatrical run, where it achieved strong per‑screen averages and a 4.4 debut score on Letterboxd, placing it among the platform’s top 70 movies. Its success has generated a record expansion from the first week, showcasing A24’s ability to turn niche films into mainstream hits.

The story follows Peter, a 30‑something Manhattan club promoter whose decade‑long party life spirals into a surreal exploration of identity, drugs, and love. After a decade of reckless living, Peter’s relationship with his best friend Sophie deteriorates, and he is drawn into a new world by influencer Edison, who reveals a hidden truth about Peter’s past. The film blends outrageous humor with poignant moments, creating a dramedy that feels both absurd and deeply human.

Critics have praised the film’s comedic timing, authentic queer representation, and strong performances. Its crowd‑pleasing nature has translated into robust word‑of‑mouth, making it a notable contender for awards season.

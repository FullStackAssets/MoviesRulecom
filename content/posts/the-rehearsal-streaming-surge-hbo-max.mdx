The surge signals that unconventional formats can still capture mass attention on premium services.

## What happened
Nathan Fielder’s two‑season series “The Rehearsal” has consistently earned Rotten Tomatoes scores no lower than 95%, and its latest streaming performance places it within FlixPatrol’s top ten for HBO Max. The climb began two weeks ago and aligns with the launch of Fielder’s newest project, which has generated fresh critical buzz. While the show’s absurd reenactments—such as staged job interviews and mock weddings—have sparked controversy with entities like Paramount and the FAA, they have also amplified audience curiosity.

## Why it matters
The surge demonstrates that HBO Max’s audience is receptive to high‑concept comedy that blurs reality and performance, encouraging the platform to invest in riskier, creator‑driven projects. For creators, it validates the commercial viability of experimental storytelling. For viewers, the spike offers an entry point to a series that rewards repeated watching and discussion.

The streaming world got a fresh political docuseries this week as Prime Video dropped the first trailer for "Melania, The Series" and confirmed its debut on Friday, November 20. The two‑episode follow‑up to Brett Ratner’s earlier documentary promises never‑before‑told moments from the early days of the Trump White House. With the trailer’s stark visuals and Melania’s own words, the series aims to deepen the narrative beyond the film’s theatrical run. For fans of documentary storytelling and political drama, the release adds a new, binge‑ready option to Prime Video’s lineup.

<Callout type="takeaway">The series arrives on Prime Video Nov 20, giving viewers a new, two‑episode look at Melania Trump’s early days in the White House.</Callout>

## What happened
Prime Video announced that "Melania, The Series" will premiere on Friday, November 20, and simultaneously released the official trailer featuring the former first lady’s reflections on power, family, and media. The series consists of two episodes—"Family & Trust" and "Pride & Honor"—which explore Melania’s priorities as she steps into the role of First Lady and the immediate actions she takes after the 47th Presidential inauguration.

The production is a joint effort by Amazon MGM Studios, Muse Films, New Element Media, and RatPac Entertainment, with Brett Ratner directing and serving as producer alongside Fernando Sulichi. Melania Trump and Marc Beckman are listed as executive producers. The series follows Ratner’s documentary, which opened theatrically on January 30 and streamed on March 9, a film that earned roughly $16 million worldwide against a $75 million budget.

## Why it matters
For streaming audiences, the series represents a rare, dramatized glimpse into a recent First Lady’s personal and political journey, expanding the narrative beyond the documentary’s limited box‑office run. Its placement on Prime Video ensures immediate accessibility, potentially drawing viewers who missed the theatrical release. The timing also highlights the growing appetite for politically charged docuseries that blend personal storytelling with historical context, positioning Amazon’s platform as a hub for contemporary political content.

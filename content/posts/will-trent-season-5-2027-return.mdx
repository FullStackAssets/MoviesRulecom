ABC has officially confirmed that the procedural drama Will Trent will return for a fifth season in 2027. The announcement comes after the show’s fourth season premiered nearly a year ago and all four seasons were recently added to Netflix, where they quickly entered the platform’s top‑10. Fans who have been waiting for news now have a concrete timeline, even though the exact premiere month remains unannounced. The delay also positions the series against other legal thrillers that dominate streaming charts.

## What happened
ABC disclosed that filming for Season 5 has already wrapped, but the network does not expect the episodes to air before the end of this calendar year. Instead, the series is slated for a 2027 broadcast, giving the production team ample time to refine scripts and visual effects.
The show’s four existing seasons were added to Netflix last week, and they surged into the platform’s top‑10 list, directly competing with Netflix’s own legal thriller, The Lincoln Lawyer. This move has broadened Will Trent’s audience beyond its original ABC viewership.
## Why it matters
By committing to a 2027 premiere, ABC signals strong confidence in the franchise’s longevity, even as the television landscape becomes increasingly fragmented between broadcast and streaming. The extended gap could risk losing some momentum, but the recent Netflix surge suggests a growing fan base that may sustain interest. Additionally, the timing places Will Trent in a favorable window to capitalize on the continued popularity of courtroom dramas without clashing with major network premieres.

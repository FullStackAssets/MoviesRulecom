Hallin was a longtime producer and occasional on‑screen performer for her brother’s hit sitcom Laverne & Shirley. She also appeared in a 1974 episode of The Odd Couple and produced several of Garry’s other shows, including Happy Days and Mork & Mindy. Her death marks the loss of a key figure in the golden era of American television.

## What happened

Hallin was born January 2, 1938, in New York City to director Anthony Masciarelli and tap‑dance teacher Marjorie Irene. She began her career with a small role in her brother’s 1974 ABC adaptation of The Odd Couple and later appeared in a 1982 episode of Laverne & Shirley as Maxine.

Hallin also produced many of Garry’s projects, from Happy Days and Blansky’s Beauties to the feature film A League of Their Own. She worked on films directed by her brother such as Frankie and Johnny, Exit to Eden, and Raising Helen. Hallin’s career spanned television and film, and she left behind three children from her marriage to Larry Hallin.

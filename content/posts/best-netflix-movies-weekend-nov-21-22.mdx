## What happened

Steps (2026) is a reimagined Cinderella story in which the stepsisters are voiced by a star‑packed cast, including Emmy winner Ali Wong, Amanda Seyfried, and Daniel Radcliffe. The film follows the stepsisters as they turn a sister into a frog and must save the kingdom, blending humor with a fresh perspective on the classic tale.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping marks the first feature‑film adaptation of Suzanne Collins’ popular series, bringing the dystopian world to the big screen for the first time. Ebenezer, a modern Charles Dickens adaptation starring Johnny Depp, has already topped box‑office charts and is now available on US Netflix, adding a literary classic to the lineup.

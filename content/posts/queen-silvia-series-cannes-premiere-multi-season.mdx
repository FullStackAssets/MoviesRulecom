The new Swedish‑German royal drama ‘Queen Silvia’ opened to a red‑carpet screening in Cannes on Tuesday. Starring Alicia von Rittberg as Silvia Sommerlath and Edvin Endre as Crown Prince Carl Gustaf, the series marks Prime Video’s first high‑profile European period piece of the year. Beta Film, the international sales agent, announced that the show will launch on Prime Video in German‑speaking markets and the Nordics, with a second‑window deal for Sweden’s SVT. The producers are already talking about a multi‑season arc, betting that the blend of romance, modern sensibility and royal intrigue will sustain audience interest beyond the initial eight‑episode run.

<Callout type="takeaway">‘Queen Silvia’ has secured a Cannes debut and is positioned for multi‑season expansion on Prime Video.</Callout>

## What happened
The Cannes premiere featured a live rendition of ABBA’s “Dancing Queen” on a grand piano, underscoring the series’ 1970s setting. The event gathered the lead actors, director Miguel Alexandre, and Beta Film executives, who highlighted the series’ cross‑border production between Germany’s Pyjama Pictures and Sweden’s Unlimited Stories.

According to Justus Riesenkampff, EVP Nordics & Benelux at Beta Film, royal love stories are performing strongly in the market and “Queen Silvia” combines classic period elements with a contemporary take on gender dynamics, making it attractive to younger viewers. He added that the series’ narrative structure leaves room for additional seasons that could explore later chapters of the royal couple’s life.

## Why it matters
The series taps into a proven appetite for royal biopics, following the success of shows like “The Crown” and “Máxima.” By anchoring the story in a modern, feminist perspective, it differentiates itself and appeals to both history buffs and younger streaming audiences. Its multi‑territorial rollout demonstrates Prime Video’s strategy to bolster regional content that can also travel internationally through Beta Film’s sales network.

## What happened

The 2019 HBO miniseries spans nine episodes, earning a 96% rating on Rotten Tomatoes and 11 Emmy wins, including Outstanding Limited Series.
It was created by Damon Lindelof and stars Regina King as Angela Abar, a Tulsa police detective who doubles as the masked vigilante Sister Night.
The adaptation stays true to the DC comic while updating the setting to a contemporary Tulsa, adding new characters and a modern tone.

## Why it matters

Watchmen’s consistent quality across all episodes sets a new benchmark for limited series, influencing how future sci‑fi dramas are produced.
Its blend of crime, thriller, and superhero elements offers a fresh take on the superhero genre, appealing to both fans and newcomers.
The series’ success demonstrates HBO’s ability to deliver high‑budget, high‑quality content that can compete with major streaming platforms.

The Mighty Nein, the Emmy‑winning adult animated series from Critical Role, is set to return to Prime Video next spring. During a TwitchCon one‑shot on November 15, the show’s creators announced that Season 2 will launch on March 24, 2027, with eight episodes released simultaneously. Fans can expect a high‑seas pirate adventure, new characters, and the continuation of the group’s evolving story. The surprise drop strategy signals Prime Video’s confidence in binge‑ready releases for niche animated titles. This announcement reshapes the streaming calendar for fantasy fans.

## What happened
On Sunday, November 15, during TwitchCon, a special actual‑play one‑shot titled *The Rings of Power: A Middle‑Earth One‑Shot* featured Critical Role cast members Sam Riegel, Travis Willingham, and Liam O'Brien alongside *Rings of Power* actors Maxim Baldry and Benjamin Walker. In that event, O'Brien, who served as game master, announced that the Emmy‑award‑winning series will return for Season 2 on March 24, 2027, with eight new episodes released in a single drop.

The first season concluded with the core group—Jester Lavorre, Fjord Stone, Nott the Brave, Caleb, Beauregard Lionett, Mollymauk Tealeaf, and newcomer Yasha Nydoorin—fully assembled. Season 2 promises a pirate‑themed storyline that takes the party across the high seas, new faces from the original tabletop campaign, and the ongoing threat of the Beacon.

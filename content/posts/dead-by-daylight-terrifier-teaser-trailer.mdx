Dead by Daylight has just dropped a new teaser trailer introducing the Terrifier, the latest killer to join its roster. The short video gives fans a first glimpse of the masked murderer’s brutal aesthetic and his unique power set. The trailer confirms that Terrifier will arrive on November 17 across every platform the game supports. This addition expands the game’s asymmetrical horror formula and raises expectations for upcoming seasonal updates.
<Callout type="takeaway">Terrifier’s debut promises fresh terror and new gameplay dynamics for Dead by Daylight.</Callout>
## What happened
The official teaser was released on IGN’s video channel, showcasing the Terrifier’s silhouette, blood‑splattered mask, and a chilling in‑game animation. Behaviour Interactive confirmed that the new killer is a “vicious” addition, designed to heighten the tension for both survivors and killers. Terrifier is slated to launch on November 17 for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam.
## Why it matters
Adding Terrifier diversifies the killer pool, forcing survivors to adapt to a fresh set of terror mechanics and keeping the meta evolving. The cross‑platform release ensures the entire player base experiences the update simultaneously, which can boost community engagement and subscription metrics. For the horror‑gaming market, the move reinforces Dead by Daylight’s position as a flagship multiplayer scare experience.

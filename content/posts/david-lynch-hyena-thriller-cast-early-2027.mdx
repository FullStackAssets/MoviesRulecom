The long‑awaited thriller ‘Hyena,’ one of the final projects linked to the late David Lynch, has just been unveiled at the American Film Market. The film will be exec‑produced by Lynch’s estate and directed by Scott Coffey, with a cast that includes Kiernan Shipka, D’Pharaoh Woon‑Tai, Edgar Ramírez and Rosemarie DeWitt. Production is scheduled to begin in Spokane, Washington in early 2027. The announcement signals a rare post‑Lynch thriller that could shape the indie thriller landscape.

## What happened
The psychological thriller ‘Hyena’ is heading to the American Film Market with sales handled by Upgrade and WME Independent. Scott Coffey, who appeared in Lynch’s ‘Mulholland Drive,’ will direct and co‑write the screenplay with novelist Blair Mastbaum. The project also lists Oscar‑nominated producer David Lancaster as an executive producer.

The ensemble features Kiernan Shipka as Genevieve, D’Pharaoh Woon‑Tai as her enigmatic boyfriend Alexei, alongside Edgar Ramírez and Rosemarie DeWitt. Filming is slated to start in early 2027 in Spokane, Washington. The story follows Genevieve’s attempt to introduce Alexei to her family, only for his presence to unearth buried secrets and force a harrowing sacrifice.

"Hyena is a very personal film. I’ve always believed the most unsettling horror isn’t supernatural — it’s the moment grief and love turn feral, when we stop pretending we aren’t wild," Coffey said.

## Why it matters
‘Hyena’ represents one of the last films tied to David Lynch’s legacy, offering fans a glimpse of his aesthetic through a new director. The involvement of high‑profile talent like Shipka and Woon‑Tai raises the project’s visibility, while the Spokane location signals a growing trend of indie productions moving outside traditional hubs. Success could encourage more posthumous collaborations and bolster the psychological thriller niche on streaming platforms.

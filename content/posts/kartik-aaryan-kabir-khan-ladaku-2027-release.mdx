Bollywood’s rising star Kartik Aaryan is set to headline Kabir Khan’s next cinematic venture, ‘Ladaku’. The drama, described as a heart‑tugging, inspirational family entertainer, will see Aaryan also step into production through his newly launched Dopamine Studios. Birla Studios and Kabir Khan Entertainment are co‑producing, and the film is slated for a worldwide theatrical rollout on June 18, 2027. The pairing follows their successful collaboration on ‘Chandu Champion’, signaling a high‑profile release that could shape the industry’s 2027 slate.

<Callout type="takeaway">‘Ladaku’ marks Kartik Aaryan’s debut as a producer and promises a globally timed release in mid‑2027.</Callout>

## What happened

Deadline reports that Kabir Khan, known for socially resonant blockbusters such as ‘Bajrangi Bhaijaan’ and ‘83’, will direct ‘Ladaku’, a story centered on ambition, grit and hope. Birla Studios, led by Ananya Birla, confirmed the film’s production schedule and the June 18, 2027 worldwide release.

Aaryan, who earned the Filmfare Best Actor award for ‘Chandu Champion’ in 2024, will not only star but also produce the film via Dopamine Studios, a venture he launched earlier this year. The project is positioned as another emotional, family‑entertainer in the vein of Khan’s previous works.

While plot specifics remain limited, producers describe the narrative as an uplifting tale of characters battling odds through sheer willpower, echoing the thematic tone of Khan’s earlier films.

## Why it matters

The collaboration combines Aaryan’s box‑office momentum with Khan’s storytelling pedigree, creating a potential marquee event for both domestic and overseas markets. Aaryan’s producer debut adds a layer of creative control that could influence the film’s tone and marketing strategy. Moreover, a worldwide release in mid‑2027 positions ‘Ladaku’ to compete in a crowded international slate, potentially boosting Bollywood’s global visibility and shaping streaming acquisition negotiations later.

The Switch remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time just celebrated its debut with a 9.6 user score on Metacritic, instantly becoming one of the year's top-rated titles. Within days of launch, data miners uncovered duplicate files labeled "MQ", pointing to a forthcoming Master Quest expansion. Unlike previous Zelda DLC that unlocks through gameplay, this new content appears to be a paid add‑on. The discovery has ignited speculation about fresh puzzles, new areas, and even quirky additions like cows in Jabu-Jabu's Belly. For longtime fans, the prospect of revisiting Hyrule with a fresh twist is hard to ignore.

## What happened
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake launched on Nintendo Switch last week and quickly earned a 9.6 user score on Metacritic, making it the highest‑rated game of the year so far. Shortly after release, community data miners analyzing the game’s code found numerous duplicate assets labeled "MQ", a clear reference to the classic Master Quest mode.

These files suggest a separate DLC package rather than an in‑game unlock, and early reports from user OatmealDome on X indicate that the new Master Quest will mirror the GameCube version while adding novel elements such as "cows in Jabu-Jabu's Belly". The content appears incomplete, implying additional updates will follow the DLC’s official launch.

Nintendo has not issued a formal announcement, though series producer Eiji Aonuma was asked about Master Quest in a recent IGN interview and offered no definitive confirmation, leaving fans to rely on the data‑mined evidence.

## Why it matters
A paid Master Quest DLC could extend the lifespan of an already celebrated remake, driving further sales and keeping the Switch library fresh. It also signals Nintendo’s willingness to monetize classic content, a trend that may influence future releases. For players, the new challenges and whimsical additions promise a fresh way to experience a beloved adventure, while the lack of official confirmation adds a layer of uncertainty about pricing and release timing.

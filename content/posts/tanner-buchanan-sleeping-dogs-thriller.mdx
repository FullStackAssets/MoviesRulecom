Tanner Buchanan, best known for his role as Robby Keene on Netflix's *Cobra Kai*, has just been announced as the lead in the upcoming psychological thriller *The Sleeping Dogs of Southampton*. The film also stars Dylan Sprouse and Barbie Ferreira, forming a trio of young talent poised to tackle a story of sex, money, and obsession set in the Hamptons. This casting move signals Buchanan’s shift from martial‑arts drama to a darker, corporate‑focused narrative, a transition that could broaden his appeal beyond the series’ fan base. For viewers who followed his work on *Designated Survivor* and earlier thriller films, the new role offers a chance to see him in a more serious, adult context.

<Callout type="takeaway">Buchanan’s move from *Cobra Kai* to a high‑stakes thriller marks a pivotal career pivot.</Callout>

## What happened
Variety reported that Tanner Buchanan, Dylan Sprouse, and Barbie Ferreira have officially signed on to lead *The Sleeping Dogs of Southampton*. Buchanan will portray Henry Barrett, a hyper‑competitive banker drawn into a lethal game of corporate and psychological chess. Sprouse plays the enigmatic Wall Street rainmaker Dorsey Grace, while Ferreira is cast as Barrett’s wife, Lindsay, whose connection to Grace fuels the film’s tension.

The movie is described as a psychological thriller that immerses the audience in the “dangerous world of sex, money, and obsession.” Set over a summer in the Hamptons, the plot follows Barrett’s descent as he navigates Manhattan’s elite while confronting a rival who seems linked to his own marriage. Production details remain under wraps, but the casting announcement confirms the project is moving forward.

## Why it matters
Buchanan’s transition highlights a broader trend of streaming‑era actors leveraging genre‑specific fame to secure more diverse roles. Moving from a martial‑arts drama to a corporate thriller could expand his credibility with casting directors looking for range. The film also brings together three actors with distinct fan bases—*Cobra Kai* followers, Disney Channel alumni, and *Euphoria* enthusiasts—potentially widening its audience. For streaming platforms, the project offers fresh, adult‑oriented content that can attract viewers seeking suspense over action.

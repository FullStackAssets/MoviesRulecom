The Awakening has just landed on major streaming platforms. Clocking in at 96 minutes, the film follows an elite unit deep in the Colombian jungle on a covert mission. What begins as a tactical operation quickly devolves into a nightmarish battle against a primeval force that once doomed a Spanish conquistador expedition five centuries ago. Its blend of war‑movie intensity and creature‑feature horror positions it as a timely alternative for fans waiting on the next Predator installment.

## What happened

An elite military squad is dispatched to eliminate a high‑value target hidden in the dense Colombian rainforest. The operation turns chaotic when the team encounters a mysterious beast that defies modern weaponry, echoing a curse that plagued a Spanish expedition in the 1500s.

The film is directed by Jaime Osorio Marquez, with co‑direction by Federico Durán and José Luis Rugeles, and features a cast that includes Claudio Cataño, Alejandro Buitrago, Héctor Sánchez, Andrea Olaya, and Ricardo Leguizamo. Distributed by Well Go USA, the trailer released this week showcases the jungle’s claustrophobic atmosphere and the creature’s relentless aggression.

By linking the modern soldiers’ struggle to the historic conquistador tragedy, The Awakening adds a mythic layer that differentiates it from typical Predator entries.

Streaming services are hungry for fresh genre hybrids that can capture both action fans and horror enthusiasts. As the Predator franchise pauses, this film fills a content gap, potentially shaping future streaming acquisitions toward more blended war‑horror projects.

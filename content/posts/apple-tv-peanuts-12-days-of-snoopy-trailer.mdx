With an all‑star musical lineup that includes John Legend, Kamasi Washington and Laufey, the trailer hints at a fresh blend of nostalgia and contemporary sound. Streaming fans will see the special debut exclusively on Apple TV, expanding the platform’s growing library of Peanuts content. The buzz around the trailer signals strong seasonal interest for both longtime fans and new viewers.

## What happened
The streaming service released the official trailer for “Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy,” a new animated Christmas special that pits Snoopy against a self‑imposed naughty‑list crisis. Set during the final twelve days before Christmas, the plot follows Snoopy as he enlists his Peanuts friends to win Santa’s favor and restore his good‑list status.

Emmy‑nominated composer Jeff Morrow scored the special, while the soundtrack features original songs from John Legend (“Get Ready”), Kamasi Washington with Leon Bridges (“Trying to Get There”), and a holiday rendition by Laufey produced by Spencer Stewart. The trailer showcases these musical moments alongside classic Peanuts humor, confirming the special’s exclusive debut on Apple TV.

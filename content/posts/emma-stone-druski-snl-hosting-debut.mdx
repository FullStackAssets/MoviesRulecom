Druski, the viral comedian known for his TikTok sketches, made history on Saturday Night Live last night. He opened his monologue by noting he is the first internet content creator to ever host the show. The highlight came when Oscar‑winner Emma Stone stepped onto the stage to join him, referencing the two’s recent film collaboration. Their joint appearance underscores how streaming stars are crossing over into traditional TV events, reshaping audience expectations.

<Callout type="takeaway">Druski’s SNL debut, bolstered by Emma Stone’s cameo, signals the growing clout of digital creators in legacy TV.</Callout>

## What happened
Druski, real name Drew Desbordes, opened his first SNL episode with a monologue that emphasized his status as the inaugural online influencer host, joking about TikTok, YouTube and Instagram fame.

Mid‑show, Emma Stone entered the stage, greeting Druski and joking about their recent movie “The Catch,” where they had not been love interests but the sketch playfully suggested a new dynamic.

The host also laid out the few SNL rules he would follow—no cussing and no “whiteface”—while thanking his family in the audience and reflecting on his journey from a suburban kid to a mainstream comedy figure.

## Why it matters
The event illustrates the blurring lines between digital platforms and network television, giving creators like Druski a pathway to broader mainstream exposure. It also demonstrates how established film talent such as Emma Stone can lend credibility to emerging internet personalities, potentially influencing casting decisions and streaming deals. For streaming watchers, the crossover hints at more collaborative promotions between movies and live TV events, which could affect how new releases are marketed.
